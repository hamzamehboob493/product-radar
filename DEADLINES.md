# Deadlines calendar

Dated rules, platform changes and shutdowns that force businesses to act. Each run adds new ones and re-checks the ones 2 to 12 months out. Shipping about 3 months before a deadline is the edge; late copies earn little.

Seeded 2026-09-25 from the first three radar runs; sources are in those briefs and linked here.
