## A. Track A (Micro): No idea passed the research threshold this run

What blocked it:

1. **Fast channels are scarce.** Review times checked this run:
   - WordPress.org plugins: "anywhere between 1 and 10 days", target 5 business days ([WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/developers/add/)) [F]. Fits Track A.
   - Woo Marketplace: "a formal decision from a reviewer within 30 days" after business review ([Woo developer docs](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/woo-marketplace/submitting-your-product)) [F]. Fails Track A.
   - Chrome Web Store: "most ... within a few days, but it can take up to a few weeks" ([Chrome docs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/review-process)) [F]. New accounts are limited to 2 extensions by default from 2026-08-20 ([Chrome blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-review-updates-2026)) [F].
   - HubSpot Marketplace: at least 3 active installs from unrelated accounts, first review "within 10 business days", and apps on platform 2025.2 are capped at 25 installs until listed ([requirements](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/apps/developer-platform/list-apps/listing-your-app/app-marketplace-listing-requirements), [install caps](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog/new-marketplace-distribution-app-install-limits)) [F].
   - Pipedrive Marketplace: review "up to 21 business days" ([Pipedrive docs](https://pipedrive.readme.io/docs/marketplace-app-approval-process)) [F]; developers report 4+ weeks in 2026 ([dev community](https://devcommunity.pipedrive.com/t/marketplace-app-review-timeline-2-weeks-no-updates-yet/20214)) [R]. Private apps can be shared by an unlisted link without review ([Pipedrive docs](https://pipedrive.readme.io/docs/marketplace-registering-a-private-app)) [F], but that gives no built-in buyers.
   - Google Workspace add-ons: review "typically takes several days" plus OAuth verification ([Google](https://developers.google.com/workspace/marketplace/about-app-review)) [F]; one report of verification stuck 8+ weeks [R].
   - Etsy API commercial access: "every try takes 20+ days" ([GitHub discussion](https://github.com/etsy/open-api/discussions/1060)) [R].
2. **The micro ideas with fast channels had no paying market.** The Shopify script-tag scanner is given away free by agencies, and the paid window closes on 2027-03-01. The Bing AI Performance exporter's free incumbent has 38 users. See part C.
3. **The micro ideas with a paying market already have free, well-rated incumbents.** Examples: WooCommerce Meta/Google feed plugins, Arabic ad-copy tools, HubSpot deal cloners plus HubSpot's free contracts feature.

## B. Track B (Bigger build): No idea passed the research threshold this run

What blocked it:

1. **Pipedrive bulk dedupe ("Koalify for Pipedrive") was the strongest proven model, but the wedge is already sold.**
   - Model is proven: Koalify (HubSpot only) has 5,000+ installs and 4.9 stars from 128 reviews ([HubSpot listing](https://ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/listing/koalify-io)) [F]. A 3-star review from 2026-08-27 says "Paying 900-1.100€ a month for a deduping tool is ridiculous" [F].
   - Gap is real: native Pipedrive merge "only detects exact matches using the persons/organization names" and "doesn't let you bulk merge duplicates" ([Datablist](https://www.datablist.com/how-to/bulk-merge-pipedrive-duplicates)) [R]. Merges "can't be un-merged" ([Pipedrive KB](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/merge-duplicates)) [F].
   - But Dedupely already covers it on Pipedrive: 300+ installs, 4.5 stars from 122 reviews, bulk, automatic and custom merges, custom-field matching ([Pipedrive listing](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/marketplace/app/dedupely/daeafd7d3dd9c2a2)) [F], at $40 a month for up to 30,000 records with all features ([pricing](https://dedupe.ly/pricing)) [F]. Insycle is free up to 5,000 records on Pipedrive ([Insycle](https://www.insycle.com/pricing/)) [F]. Our remaining wedge would be "free tier plus better UX", which G4 rules out. Marketplace review (up to 21 business days) also puts G5/G7 at risk.
2. **The WooCommerce to TikTok Shop gap closed before we arrived.**
   - Platform change is real: the official TikTok plugin (200,000+ installs, 1.8 stars) stopped new Shop connections on 2026-03-02 and dropped Shop features on 2026-06-01 ([WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/tiktok-for-business/), [ChannelUnity](https://www.channelunity.com/2026/03/02/tiktok-shop-woocommerce-plugin-support-ending/)) [F].
   - But the flat, cheap wedge we planned is taken: FLUF Connect sells Woo to TikTok Shop crosslisting from £9 a month, UK only ([FLUF](https://fluf.io/crosslisting/woocommerce-to-tiktokshop/)) [F]. M2E charges $19 to $399 a month by GMV and covers the US, UK and EU markets ([M2E](https://m2ecloud.com/woocommerce-tiktok)) [F]. ChannelUnity, LitCommerce and CedCommerce also sell it [F].
   - Unresolved: TikTok's US Partner Center is described as "for US-registered companies" [R], and the partner onboarding pages only rendered navigation [U].
3. **Arabic/RTL client reporting for GCC agencies had a clear product gap but no demand evidence.** AgencyAnalytics supports 7 languages and no Arabic ([language page](https://agencyanalytics.com/features/language-options)) [F]. The only buyer-side signal found was one Power BI forum thread asking for RTL embedding ([Fabric community](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Service/Request-for-RTL-Support-When-Embedding-Reports-in-Arabic-for-GCC/td-p/4367113)) [F]. A Meta Ad Library search for "client reporting dashboard agencies" in AE and SA returned 32 ads, none from a GCC-focused reporting SaaS [F]. That is fewer than three independent buyer signals and no spending signal, so it fails G2.
4. **Sales and marketing categories are crowded with free native features.** HubSpot Revenue Hub Free now includes contracts and subscriptions ([HubSpot pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/revenue)) [F]. Pipedrive has native WhatsApp on Growth and above ([Pipedrive KB](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/whatsapp-integration)) [F]. DMARC monitoring has fully free tiers from Valimail and Cloudflare ([EmailVendorSelection](https://www.emailvendorselection.com/best-dmarc-monitoring-tools/)) [R].
5. **TrustMRR's Sales category is dominated by outreach and lead-gen tools** ([TrustMRR Sales](https://trustmrr.com/category/sales)) [F], which Polar and Creem refuse. The Marketing category's top earners are agencies and influencer platforms, not self-serve tools ([TrustMRR Marketing](https://trustmrr.com/category/marketing)) [F].

## D. Platform shifts

1. **HubSpot ends support for all v1 to v3 APIs and legacy apps, enforced September 2027** (announced 2026-09-15). Pipelines API v1 stops on 2026-12-04, and legacy private app creation is being disabled ([HubSpot changelog](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog/legacy-apis-and-legacy-apps-whats-going-unsupported-and-when), [Pipelines v1](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog/pipelines-api-v1-sunset)) [F]. Opens: fixed-price migration services for custom HubSpot integrations over the next 12 months, not a product.
2. **HubSpot enforces admin validation rules on all CRM API writes from API version 2026-09** (posted 2026-08-11) ([HubSpot changelog](https://developers.hubspot.com/changelog)) [F]. Opens: integrations that write bad data will start failing; a write-error monitor for HubSpot integrations may be worth screening when sector 3 comes round again.
3. **TikTok's WooCommerce plugin dropped TikTok Shop on 2026-06-01** ([ChannelUnity](https://www.channelunity.com/2026/03/02/tiktok-shop-woocommerce-plugin-support-ending/)) [F]. Opens: little now; third-party connectors moved in within weeks. Same lesson as the EU compliance apps: be early or skip.
4. **Shopify script tags can no longer be created or updated from 2026-10-01, and stop running 2027-03-01** ([Shopify changelog](https://shopify.dev/changelog/online-store-script-tags-deprecation)) [F]. Opens: agency migration work for merchants on abandoned apps; the free audit is a lead magnet, not a product.
