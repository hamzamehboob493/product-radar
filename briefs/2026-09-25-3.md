## A. Track A (Micro): No idea passed the research threshold this run

Screened for Track A: Query-Language Guard for Google Ads, Figma design context for coding agents, WhatsApp Cloud API spend and username guardrail, Jobber bulk price increases, Apify Yelp Actor. General blockers:

1. **Fresh platform changes have no paying buyers yet.** Google's language-targeting removal is only starting to roll out. The only buyer voice found is one unnamed insurance advertiser at a Google roundtable, reported by SEJ [R]. Google says "No action is required by advertisers" and that ads will match the user's query language [F] ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722078?hl=en)). The Search Terms report also hides low-volume terms for privacy [F] ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472708?hl=en)), so any "spend by language" report would be partial.
2. **Free scripts and open-source bridges sit next to every micro idea.** Shabba publishes free GPL Google Ads scripts, including LLM auto-negatives ([GitHub](https://github.com/charlesbannister/free-google-ads-scripts)) [F]. Flaude already sells a Figma plugin bridge for MCP at $18/mo and pitches it for free accounts ([Flaude](https://www.flaude.app/figma-mcp-alternative)) [F].
3. **The marketplace with instant listing (Apify) is thin on non-lead-gen demand.** The Yelp leader has about 169 monthly users at 2.70 stars [F] ([Apify API](https://api.apify.com/v2/store?limit=30&search=yelp)), and Yelp has a history of acting against scrapers [I].

Near miss worth re-checking: **Query-Language Guard, mid-November 2026**, once practitioners have post-rollout data. Cheap test then: publish a free script that tags search terms by language into a Sheet, post it to r/PPC and LinkedIn, and pitch PPC Land. Go only with 150+ installs in 14 days, 5+ unprompted replies quoting wrong-language spend, and 3+ people prepaying $29 on Polar.

## B. Track B (Bigger build): No idea passed the research threshold this run

Screened for Track B: UK vet CMA compliance kit, HubSpot integration heartbeat, England PRS registration pack, Google Docs/Slides accessibility fixer, ZATCA Phase 2 for WooCommerce, Shopmonkey KPI digest, Atlassian Connect to Forge rebuild. What blocked them:

1. **The system of record ships the feature.**
   - Vets: the CMA order is final (made 22 Sep 2026). Smaller businesses (fewer than 15 practices) get about 6 months for price and complaints remedies and about 12 months for written estimates (£500+ incl. VAT), itemised bills and the prescription fee cap (£21 first item, £12.50 each additional) [F] ([CMA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-veterinary-businesses-and-vets-need-to-do-following-the-cmas-final-vets-report)). But Provet already has e-signed estimates, itemised invoices and e-signed written prescriptions, and is building the fee cap into billing [F] ([Provet](https://www.provet.com/uk-cma-reforms)). Pricebook.vet gives CMA price lists away "free forever" [F] ([pricing](https://pricebook.vet/pricing)), the CMA and RCVS will supply the prescription materials [F], and VetComply is pre-selling a £29/mo compliance dashboard for 1 to 5 vet independents [F] ([VetComply](https://vetcomply.co.uk/)).
   - HubSpot: the Connected Apps page on all plans already shows daily created, updated, deleted and merged record counts per app, plus disconnect and error alerts [F] ([HubSpot KB](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/integrations/manage-your-connected-apps)). The "what will break" inventory cannot be built, because the Property Validations API does not expose conditional-required or required-on-create rules [F] ([API guide](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/crm-property-validations-v3/guide)).
2. **Cheap specialists already sell the wedge.**
   - PRS database: LetCompliance already sells a "PRS Database readiness tracker" on every paid plan (£7.99 to £129/mo) [F] ([pricing](https://letcompliance.com/pricing)); LLCR has an agency tier at £39/mo plus £1 per property [F] ([LLCR](https://www.llcr.uk/)); Goodlord says it will expose held compliance data for registration [F] ([Goodlord, 10 Sep 2026](https://blog.goodlord.co/the-government-confirms-register-your-rental-property-service-what-landlords-and-letting-agents-need-to-know)). Agents cannot register for landlords [F] ([gov.uk](https://housinghub.campaign.gov.uk/renting-is-changing/get-ready-to-register/)).
   - Docs accessibility: Inkable already ships AI alt text, one-click fixes and PDF/UA at $15/mo with a free tier, and markets on the Title II deadline [F] ([Inkable pricing](https://inkabledocs.com/pricing)). Grackle Docs has 7M+ installs [F] ([listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/grackle_docs/1085622905455)). Some universities block add-ons on managed accounts [F] ([UMN](https://it.umn.edu/services-technologies/how-tos/google-docs-use-accessibility-checker)).
   - Shopmonkey KPI digest: AutoRx already sells Tekmetric and Shopmonkey KPI reports at $50/mo per shop [F] ([AutoRx](https://autorx.app/)); Tekmetric's API needs partner approval of 2 to 3 weeks, "not guaranteed" [R] ([Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/tekmetric-ai/)).
3. **A regulation deadline without buyers.** ZATCA Wave 25 is real, but the only paid WooCommerce plugin on CodeCanyon, FatooraPro, shows 3 sales at $104 (updated 14 Jul 2026) [F] ([CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/search/zatca)), and the free WordPress.org plugins have fewer than 10 installs [F] ([Furat](https://wordpress.org/plugins/furat-for-zatca-integration/)). Salla gives its ZATCA app away free [F] ([Salla](https://apps.salla.sa/en/app/747360824)).
4. **Payout rail unverified.** Atlassian Marketplace partner payouts to Pakistan are undocumented; a developer asked for the country list on 9 Aug 2026 and got no answer [F] ([Atlassian community](https://community.developer.atlassian.com/t/supported-countries-for-marketplace-partner-registration-and-payouts-where-is-this-documented/102084)).
