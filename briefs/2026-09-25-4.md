### 1. Complaints log plus CMA compliance tracker for UK independent vets: 70 (13 · 14 · 12 · 9 · 9 · 8 · 5)

- **Buyer:** UK independent vet practices (fewer than 15 sites), usually the practice manager.
- **Why now:** the CMA order was made on 22 Sep 2026. Smaller businesses get about 6 months for the price and complaints remedies [F].
- **Money proof:**
  - VetComply has a waitlist for a compliance dashboard for 1 to 5 vet independents, with planned pricing of £29/month [F]. That shows a vendor expects paying demand; it doesn't prove sales.
  - Provet is building CMA features into its paid practice-management software [F].
- **Angle:**
  - Provet states "There is no complaints module in Provet" [F].
  - A practice-software-agnostic complaints log would track the statutory clocks (5-working-day acknowledgement, 8-week response), with the CMA checklist and deadline tracker on top.
  - It needs a check that ezyVet, RxWorks and Teleos also lack complaints handling [U].
- **Channel:** direct email and LinkedIn to practice managers, and UK vet business groups. Trust from a Pakistan-based team is a risk; a UK-looking brand and a clear data-protection page help.
- **Price:** £29 a month per practice, with a founding-member discount.
- **Payment:** Polar, since vet admin SaaS isn't health advice [F].
- **Biggest risk:** VetComply gets there first, or the big practice-management systems add complaints modules within 6 months.
- **Validation test (5 to 7 days, under $50):**
  - Build a one-page site describing the complaints log and CMA tracker, with a Polar pre-order at £19 a month for founding members.
  - Send 60 personalised emails to UK independent practice managers.
  - **Go:** 5 or more pre-orders, or 10 or more demo requests. **No-go:** fewer than 2 of either.

### 2. Bulk duplicate merge for Pipedrive, with merge undo: 70 (21 · 7 · 11 · 11 · 8 · 8 · 4)

- **Buyer:** Pipedrive admins at small sales teams with messy imports.
- **Money proof:**
  - Dedupely on Pipedrive: 300+ installs, 4.5 stars from 122 reviews, $40 a month [F].
  - Koalify does the same on HubSpot with 5,000+ installs and 4.9 stars [F].
  - A HubSpot user complained about "paying 900-1.100€ a month for a deduping tool" [F].
- **Pain:** Pipedrive's native merge only matches exact names, can't bulk merge, and merges "can't be un-merged" [F].
- **Angle:**
  - merge undo, by snapshotting records before every merge
  - fuzzy matching that handles non-Latin names (Arabic, Urdu)
  - a cheaper tier for teams under 5,000 records
  - The earlier run couldn't read Dedupely's review text, so check it first for undo or accuracy complaints [U].
- **Channel:**
  - Pipedrive Marketplace (review takes up to 21 business days, so launch on our own site first)
  - Pipedrive community
  - Pipedrive consultants on LinkedIn
- **Price:** $19 to $39 a month.
- **Payment:** Polar on our own site.
- **Biggest risk:** Dedupely is good enough for most buyers, and undo isn't valued enough to switch.
- **Validation test (5 to 7 days, under $100):**
  - Spend 1 hour reading Dedupely and Insycle reviews.
  - Build a landing page ("Dedupe Pipedrive without fear: every merge can be undone") with Polar pre-orders.
  - Post in the Pipedrive community and send 30 DMs to Pipedrive consultants.
  - **Go:** 5 or more pre-orders, or 25 or more waitlist signups. **No-go:** under 10 signups.
