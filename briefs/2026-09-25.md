## A. Track A (Micro): No idea passed the research threshold this run

What blocked it:

1. **The Apify Store is saturated in every niche we are strong in.** Bayut has 10+ Actors, Property Finder 9+, Dubizzle 8+, US contractor licences and permits 20+ combined, Rightmove 10, Zameen 7+, US state bars 6+. Most were updated within the last month [F, individual Actor pages]. The best monthly-user figure found was 34 ([memo23/domain-com-au-scraper](https://apify.com/memo23/domain-com-au-scraper)). The leading Bayut Actor has 6 monthly users ([dhrumil/bayut-scraper](https://apify.com/dhrumil/bayut-scraper)) [F]. At $1 to $25 per 1,000 results, a new entrant would likely earn tens of dollars a month [I]. That fails G4 (no evidence-based wedge) and G8.
2. **Some data is free at the source.** California publishes its full contractor licence list as a free download ([CSLB data portal](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/onlineservices/dataportal/ContractorList.aspx)) [F]. Permit Actors mostly repackage free city open-data APIs [F].
3. **Shopify can't be used to launch by day 10.** Shopify staff said on 2026-02-26 that "review times are longer than usual" ([community.shopify.dev](https://community.shopify.dev/t/longer-app-store-review-times-what-you-need-to-know/31728)) [F]. Developers report waits of 3 to 4+ weeks ([thread](https://community.shopify.dev/t/whats-the-current-timeframe-for-app-reviews/32649)) [R]. That fails G7 for Track A.
4. **Apify payouts to Pakistan are unverified.** Apify pays 80% of fees minus platform costs ([terms](https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions)) via PayPal, Wise or SWIFT wire ([payouts](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize/monthly-payouts)) [F]. No Apify page lists eligible countries [U]. PayPal is not available in Pakistan. Confirm Wise or wire receipt before any Apify-based pick.

**Worth trying next run:** instead of scraping portals, look for Actors whose data is not free, with visible buyer requests in Apify Store issues or ideas threads. This needs direct access to the Store API, which the network proxy blocked this run.

## B. Track B (Bigger build): No idea passed the research threshold this run

What blocked it:

1. **EU compliance apps turn into commodities within weeks of each deadline.**
   - GPSR: 11+ Shopify apps, several free. The leader, [GCM GPSR](https://apps.shopify.com/gpsr), has 29 reviews after two years [F].
   - EU withdrawal button: [Revoq](https://apps.shopify.com/eu-withdrawal-form) reached 555 reviews at Free, $9 and $25 a month, then 8+ copies followed, including a lifetime-deal one [F].
   - EU legal-guarantee label (from 27 Sep 2026): about 10 mostly free apps ([Rankbase](https://rankbase.io/best-shopify-apps/gewahrleistungslabel)) [R].
   - Lesson [I]: regulation-driven apps win only if they ship about 3 months before the deadline.
2. **Incumbents now include features for free.**
   - GoHighLevel has a native Housecall Pro integration ([changelog](https://ideas.gohighlevel.com/changelog/housecall-pro-more-workflow-actions-triggers)) [F]. Housecall Pro's API is limited to its MAX plan ([help](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8505035-api-overview)) [F].
   - Shopify reportedly moved key B2B features to all plans in April 2026 and added Agentic Storefronts for AI shopping channels [R].
3. **The GCC and WhatsApp angles are crowded.**
   - COD confirmation on Shopify has 15+ apps. The leading COD forms, [Releasit](https://apps.shopify.com/releasit-cod-order-form) (2,499 reviews) and [EasySell](https://apps.shopify.com/easy-order-form) (963 reviews, $9.95 to $59.95 a month), already include WhatsApp or OTP [F].
   - The Saudi National Address niche already has four 2026 apps with 0 to 2 reviews each ([Onwan](https://apps.shopify.com/national-address), [Enwany](https://apps.shopify.com/enwany)) [F]. That means no proven paid leader, which fails G3.
4. **The one candidate with a thin field could not be de-risked from desk research.** See the HubSpot UAE portals line in part C.

  - HubSpot partner agencies build this as custom work: [Digital Nexa](https://www.digitalnexa.com/hubspot-services/integrations/property-finder-integration) and [Ubrik](https://www.ubrik.com/hubspot/hubspot-and-property-finder-integration) [F].
  - A Freelancer.com job for a HubSpot to Property Finder/Bayut integration, budget £18 to 36 an hour, posted about 2 years ago ([job](https://www.freelancer.com/projects/hubspot/rest-api-developer-for-hubspot)) [F].

  - An adjacent model has verified revenue: ImmoWP, a French listing sync, $45,899 all time per [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/immowp) [F].
- **Evidence against it:**
  - Albato already offers a Bayut to HubSpot lead sync with a free plan ([Albato](https://albato.com/connect/bayut-with-hubspot)) [F], so importing leads is not a wedge.
  - Property Finder runs a partner program, the [Developer Network](https://pfdn.propertyfinder.com/) [F], but the portal and its integration guide did not render, and the help centre blocks robots. Whether a third-party vendor can get access, and on what terms, is **[U]**.
  - Most Dubai brokers probably use vertical CRMs that include portal sync (for example [PropCRM](https://propcrm.com/pricing.php) at $55 to $85 per user a month) [I].
- **What would bring it back:**
  1. A broker-issued Property Finder Enterprise API key and a Bayut feed are confirmed to work from a third-party app.
  2. At least 5 HubSpot-using Dubai brokerages, or 2 HubSpot agencies, are found and agree to a paid pilot.

  Both are outreach tasks, so a human has to do them.

## D. Platform shifts

1. **Shopify script tags sunset.** From 2026-10-01, script tags cannot be created or updated. On 2027-03-01 they stop running ([changelog](https://shopify.dev/changelog/online-store-script-tags-deprecation), posted 2026-08-24) [F].
   - What it opens: a script-tag migration audit, or replacement app embeds for merchants whose older apps are abandoned. Worth screening next run for a Track A service or app.
2. **Apify retires rental Actors.** No new rental Actors since 2026-04-01, and full retirement on 2026-10-01. Rental Actors are excluded from Apify MCP search ([docs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize/rental)) [F].
   - What it opens: abandoned rental Actors in useful niches may fall out of search. Pay-per-event Actors can be found by AI agents through MCP.
3. **EU Empowering Consumers Directive applies from 2026-09-27.** It requires a legal-guarantee label and bans vague green claims ([Xictron](https://www.xictron.com/en/blog/empowering-consumers-directive-september-2026/)) [R].
   - What it opens: the label niche is already commoditised. The next opportunity is to map EU deadlines 3+ months out and ship early.
4. **Chrome Web Store single-purpose data rule.** Announced 2026-07-01 and enforced from 2026-08-01 ([Chrome blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/cws-policy-updates-2026)) [F].
   - What it opens: narrow, single-purpose extensions, and policy-compliance rebuilds of delisted multi-purpose ones.
