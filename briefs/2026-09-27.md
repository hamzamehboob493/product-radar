### Full cards for the top 5 (same as the Discord report)

#### 1. Companies House ID-check tracker · 67/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Legal and accounting (practice admin)
**What it is:** a UK accounting practice pastes its client company numbers and sees which directors and PSCs still have to verify their identity with Companies House, and when. The app emails reminders to those clients.
**Who pays:** UK accounting and bookkeeping practices with 50 to 1,000 client companies.
**The problem:** existing directors must verify "at the same time as they file their next annual confirmation statement, during a 12-month transition period" from 18 Nov 2025. About 6 to 7 million people are in scope. <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/companies-house-confirms-identity-verification-rollout-from-18-november-2025> Acting unverified becomes an offence. <https://changestoukcompanylaw.campaign.gov.uk/identity-verification/>
**Why now:** the transition ends about 18 Nov 2026. After that, every new director and PSC still has a 14-day window.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: ICAEW alone supervises about 10,000 firms <https://www.icaew.com/regulation/aml-supervision/our-role-as-an-aml-supervisor>
- Money: VerifyMyClient sells verification plus due and overdue alerts, "from £2 per verification" <https://verifymyclient.com/new-for-2026/>
- Competitors:
  - VerifyMyClient: per-verification pricing, with tracking bundled in
  - FigsFlow: quote only
  - Practice suites (TaxCalc, IRIS, Karbon): no ID-check tracker found (unknown)
  - A spreadsheet plus Companies House lookups, one company at a time
- Gap: a flat-price tracker for clients who verify themselves for free through GOV.UK One Login
**Our angle:** one flat monthly price for the whole client list, with no per-check fee.

**Launch plan:** Day 0: API check on 50 real companies. Days 1 to 5: validation test while building. Day 7: go or no-go. Days 8 to 9: test with 3 practices' real lists. Day 10: launch.
**First 10 customers:** AccountingWEB Any Answers, and LinkedIn practice managers. Pitch: "See which client directors haven't verified with Companies House, and when they're due. Free for 20 companies."
**Validation test (before building):** a free checker for up to 20 company numbers, plus a £19/mo founding pre-order on Polar. Post in 3 accountant communities and send 30 LinkedIn messages. 5 days, under $30.
- **Go:** 5+ pre-orders, or 15+ firms using it with 10+ email signups
- **No-go:** under 2 pre-orders and under 15 firms
**Risks:**

- VerifyMyClient or a practice suite adds a free tracker. Mitigation: none, except speed.
**Confidence:** Medium. Hard deadline and a paying rival, but no buyer quotes and a short peak.

#### 2. EU parcel product-ID (PID) checker · 65/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Logistics and e-commerce
**What it is:** upload a product CSV from Shopify, Woo, Etsy or any other shop. The tool checks every item for the 3 product IDs EU customs require from 1 Nov, flags gaps, suggests HS codes and exports a file ready for a customs broker or 3PL.
**Who pays:** US, UK and Asian online brands shipping parcels worth €150 or less into the EU, with 50 to 5,000 products.
**The problem:** EU Regulation 2026/1022 makes three IDs per item mandatory from 1 Nov 2026 <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202601022>:
- the seller's SKU
