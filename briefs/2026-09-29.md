#### 1. Third-party harassment evidence file · 66/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** HR
**What it is:** a small web app where a UK pub, shop, clinic or lettings office completes a guided risk assessment for harassment by customers and other third parties, logs incidents, records who read the policy and did training, and exports a dated evidence file.
**Who pays:** owners and ops managers of UK customer-facing SMBs with 10 to 250 staff; also HR consultants who serve them.
**The problem:** from 30 Oct 2026 employers are liable for harassment of staff by third parties, and Acas lists risk assessments, incident monitoring, policies and training as the expected steps. <https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-rights-act-2025/harassment-law-changes> The duty to take "all reasonable steps" is strict: one reasonable step not taken can mean liability. <https://www.hilldickinson.com/our-view/articles/third-party-harassment-employees-gain-new-protection-from-october-2026/> Tribunal claim time limits double to 6 months from 1 Oct. <https://www.blakemorgan.co.uk/employment-rights-act-2025-october-2026-implementation-dates-confirmed/>
**Why now:** hard date 30 Oct 2026, 31 days away. No regulations define the steps yet, so employers need to show their own record.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: 176,685 UK hospitality businesses alone (Mar 2025) <https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10333/>; all-UK employer count not checked (unknown)
- Money: EmployerKit sells a policy gap audit from £49 <https://employerkit.com/guides/third-party-harassment-employer-duty-2026>; Breathe £24/mo and myhrtoolkit £22/mo for small teams (reported) <https://madyhr.ai/compare/hr-software-uk>
- Competitors: HR suites (BrightHR, Breathe): no third-party harassment feature listed; Culture Shift Report + Support: enterprise, no public price; EHRC 8-step guide and Acas e-learning: free, but no log or export
- Gap: no cheap tool found that combines risk assessment, incident log and read receipts
**Our angle:** one job, one price, live before 30 Oct, with an evidence export a manager can hand to a tribunal lawyer.

#### 2. Subcontractor right-to-work evidence locker · 63/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)

**What it is:** a portal where a UK main contractor or labour user collects each subcontractor worker's right-to-work evidence (share-code result or certified check), tracks expiry and repeat checks, logs on-site identity re-checks, and exports an evidence pack for a Home Office request.
**Who pays:** UK main contractors, cleaning and facilities firms, logistics firms and labour providers with 20 to 500 non-employee workers.
**The problem:** from 1 Oct 2026 the illegal-working regime covers workers, individual subcontractors and gig/platform workers, with a civil penalty up to £60,000 per worker. **Why now:** in force 1 Oct 2026; no transitional relief described; final Code guidance still pending.
