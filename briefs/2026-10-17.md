### Top 5 cards

#### 1. Power BI Q&A replacement for Pro-only tenants · 62/100 · WATCHLIST

- **Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Productivity / BI
- **What it is:** A free scanner that lists every Q&A visual and Q&A synonym across your reports, plus a paid Power BI visual that answers plain-English questions on your own LLM key, so Pro-only teams keep "ask a question" after Microsoft removes Q&A.
- **Who pays:** Organisations on Power BI Pro or PPU without Fabric capacity (US, UK, EU, AU), and the BI consultants who maintain their reports.
- **The problem:** Q&A visuals "stop working and are removed" by end December 2026 [F] ([handsontek](https://m365admin.handsontek.net/retirement-power-bi-qa/)); Microsoft Learn now says February 2027 [F] ([Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/power-bi/consumer/end-user-q-and-a)). Copilot is not in PPU and needs F2+ [R] ([Fabric Community](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Service/Premium-Per-User-Copilot-Feature/m-p/4844355)). The manual fallback "loses natural language querying entirely" [R] ([Tygart](https://tygartmedia.com/power-bi-qa-dying-migration-guide-copilot-december-2026/)).
- **Why now:** retirement in 2.5 to 4 months; Microsoft says it is "reviewing the approach for Pro licenses" [F] ([SumProduct](https://sumproduct.com/blog/power-bi-blog-no-more-qa/)).
- **Market analysis:**
  - Market size: Pro-only tenants using Q&A: unknown.
  - Money flowing: F2 capacity about $155/month reserved to $263 pay-as-you-go [R] ([DataTako](https://datatako.com/tools/fabric-capacity-calculator)); Pro $14 and PPU $24 per user [R].
  - Competitors: Fabric F2 + Copilot ($155 to $263/mo, workspace must move to capacity); Creative Data LLM Chatbot (no price or ratings, uncertifiable) [F]; PBI AI Agent (BYO key, no price) [F]; manual rebuild (free, no NL).
  - The gap: no tool migrates Q&A setups (synonyms, featured questions) or targets Pro-only tenants explicitly [F for listings checked].
