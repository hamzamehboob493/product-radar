## D. Deep briefs

### D1. Track A: UK 6-month qualifying-service clock (63, WATCHLIST)

**1. Idea and buyer.**
- Name: working title "ServiceClock". Web app.
- Segment: UK payroll bureaus, bookkeepers and outsourced HR consultants serving micro and small employers (2 to 50 staff), across England, Wales and Scotland.
- Recurring workflow: know, for every worker at every client, when they reach 6 months' service, and make sure probation reviews and notes happen before that date. Start dates sit in payroll (BrightPay, Xero, Sage, Moneysoft) or rota tools (Deputy, RotaCloud).
- Who pays: the bureau or bookkeeper (resells or bundles), or a single employer.
