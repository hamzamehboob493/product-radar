- **US state privacy (1 Jan and 1 May 2027):** consent platforms move first [I].

- **Firebase Studio (22 Mar 2027):** 5 months, but Google's own migration tools come first [F].
- **UK Budget wage rates (April 2027) and Direct Debit (April 2029):** payroll software absorbs rates; 2029 is too far [I].

## D. Deep briefs

### D1. Track A: Australian ADM disclosure kit (58, WATCHLIST, returning)

**1. Idea and buyer.**
- Name: working title "ADM Register AU". Web app.
- Segment: Australian businesses covered by the APPs (generally turnover over A$3m; also health providers and some smaller entities) [F]([allitservices](https://allitservices.com.au/australias-new-automated-decision-making-privacy-rules-start-10-december-what-businesses-using-ai-must-do-now/)) that use software to score, rank, approve, price or route people: credit and payment checks, hiring filters, fraud tools, chatbots, dynamic pricing.
- Workflow: list every tool, ask each vendor what personal information it uses and what it decides, sort decisions into "solely" and "substantially and directly related", and keep the policy wording current as tools change.
- Who pays: the business (operations, legal or privacy lead), or a privacy consultant, MSP or accountant serving several clients.
- Why now: obligation applies from 10 Dec 2026; OAIC APP 1 Guidelines v2 and fact sheet out since 30 Sep [F]([Allens](https://www.allens.com.au/insights-news/insights/2026/10/mandatory-automated-decision-making-disclosure-requirements-are-coming-key-takeaways-from-the-oaic-guidance/)).
- Smallest promise: "List your tools, send each vendor one link, and get a categorised automated-decision section ready for your reviewer."

**2. Proven model and angle.**
- Reference products: privacy-policy generators (Termly, iubenda, Clym) and Australian privacy consultancies; their ADM coverage and prices were not checked this run [U]. US analogues for ADM logs (FairNow, Warden AI) show no public price [F, 17 Oct brief].
- What buyers still lack [F]: businesses wrongly assume human sign-off removes them from scope, miss staff-enabled AI, wait on slow vendor answers and copy competitors' wording ([allitservices](https://allitservices.com.au/australias-new-automated-decision-making-privacy-rules-start-10-december-what-businesses-using-ai-must-do-now/)). No buyer review quotes found [U].
- Our angle: the inventory and vendor-questionnaire step, with a categorised draft as output; never an opinion on whether the law applies.
- Copyability: high for generators adding an ADM clause; lower for the vendor-chasing workflow [I].

**3. Evidence with numbers.**
- Penalties up to A$330k for administrative failures [F, allitservices].
- Number of APP entities: unknown [U].
- Our arithmetic [E]: 60 businesses at $99 plus 10 consultants at $29/month is about $5,940 once and $290/month. Illustration only.

**4. Competitor table.**

| Option | Price | Strengths | Weaknesses (evidence) | Our entry point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Law firm or privacy consultant | Hourly [I] | Legal judgment | Cost; still needs vendor facts [I] | Feed them a ready inventory |
| Privacy-policy generators (Termly, iubenda, Clym) | Not checked [U] | Cheap policy text | ADM coverage unknown [U]; no vendor workflow [I] | Inventory plus vendor questionnaire |
| OAIC guidance and fact sheet | Free [F] | Authoritative | Guidance, not a workflow [F] | Turn guidance into a checklist |
| Spreadsheet | Free | Flexible | No vendor chasing or evidence trail [I] | Questionnaire links and dated evidence |

**5. MVP scope and effort.**
- Capabilities: (1) tool inventory with prompts for commonly missed tools (chatbots, Copilot features, HR filters); (2) vendor questionnaire by link, with reminders; (3) categorisation into the guideline's decision types with a reviewer note field; (4) draft APP 1.8 section and dated evidence pack (PDF); (5) Polar billing.
- Exclusions: legal applicability decisions, full privacy policies, other jurisdictions in v1 (California SB 947 later).
- Stack: Next.js, Node, Postgres, an LLM for summarising vendor answers into categories, PDF export.
- Hours [E]: inventory 8; questionnaire and reminders 8; categorisation and draft 8; evidence export 4; billing 3; QA 6; docs 2. About 39 hours, one engineer, 5 days.
- Maintenance about 3 h/month (guidance updates) [E].

**6. Launch plan (Track A, day 0 = idea confirmed).**
