## D. Deep briefs

### D1. Track A: UK third-party harassment evidence file (63, WATCHLIST, returning)

**1. Idea and buyer.** Web app. Buyer: UK (England, Wales, Scotland) customer-facing employers with 5 to 250 staff: pubs, cafés, restaurants, independent retail, dental and vet practices, gyms, letting agents, care agencies; plus the HR consultants who serve them. Recurring workflow: assess third-party risk per site and role, log incidents involving customers or clients, record actions taken, re-train and re-acknowledge policies, review yearly. Who pays: owner or operations manager, or the HR consultant on behalf of clients. Why now: duty in force since 30 Oct 2026 [F]. Smallest promise: "One dated record that shows the reasonable steps you took, ready if a claim arrives."

**2. Proven model and angle.** References: Saferspace (reporting app, AI triage, live dashboards) [F]; Culture Shift Report + Support (case management, analytics; demo only) [F]; Citation and Breathe HR (free templates and checklists) [F]; Hill Dickinson (training videos, risk assessments, policy suite, price on request) [F]. What customers lack: Pinsent Masons reports employers taking a "targeted approach" to third-party risk [F, partly summarised]; no source read lists a self-serve price for small employers [F]. No review quotes were found (Saferspace and Culture Shift have no reviews located) [U]. Our angle: risk assessment, incident log and sign-off in one place at a public price, with a QR code staff can scan to log an incident in under a minute. Copyability: high; an HR suite (BrightHR, Breathe) could add a module [I].

**3. Evidence with numbers.** NHS staff reported almost 24,000 incidents of sexual assault and harassment by patients and the public between 2022 and 2025 [F]([Culture Shift](https://culture-shift.co.uk/resources/third-party-harassment-toolkit)). Personnel Today reports UK shop workers face abuse every week [R]([Personnel Today](https://www.personneltoday.com/hr/uk-shopworkers-face-barrage-of-abuse-every-week)). Counts of UK hospitality and retail employers were not checked this run [U]. Bottom-up [E]: if 0.1% of an assumed 200,000 small customer-facing UK employers bought, that is 200 customers; the base figure is an assumption, not a source.

### D2. Track B: EUDR statement desk for small EU importers (53, WATCHLIST)
