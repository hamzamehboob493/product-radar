### D1. Track A: UK marketplace VAT per-platform records tracker (59, WATCHLIST)

**1. Idea and buyer.** Web app. UK-established sellers selling goods on two or more marketplaces (Amazon UK, eBay UK, Etsy, TikTok Shop, OnBuy), both VAT-registered and those near £90k on a single platform, and the ecommerce bookkeepers who serve them. Recurring workflow: every month, settlement reports from each platform must be reconciled, and from October 2027 the seller must know which sales the marketplace accounted VAT on and how close each platform is to its threshold. Who pays: the seller (£12/month) or the accountant (multi-client). Why now: policy published 28 Oct 2026 [F]. Smallest differentiated promise: "Upload each platform's monthly report; see your rolling total per platform against £90k and a tagged ledger of marketplace-accounted sales for your bookkeeper." It records and classifies; it gives no tax advice.

**2. Proven model and angle.** Link My Books (613 reviews, 4.99 on Xero UK) [F], A2X (from $29 [R]) and Taxomate (from $19 [R]) prove sellers pay $19 to $29 a month for marketplace-to-ledger sync. Their public pages say nothing about per-platform thresholds or marketplace-deemed sales [F for Link My Books pricing page; R for the others]. eBay's UK GM is lobbying for small-seller protection [F]([Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/news/uk/online-tax-crackdown-must-not-unfairly-hit-small-uk-businesses-ebay-boss-says-NFMCVTPTBNIA3I2NW2NI7Q3JTY)), which shows the change is noticed. Angle: a narrow, cheap, CSV-first tracker that also serves sellers who do not use any sync tool. Copy risk: high; each incumbent could ship a threshold view in weeks [I].

**3. Evidence with numbers.**
- About 100,000 businesses "significantly" affected; +£165m in 2027-28 [F]([policy paper](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vat-online-marketplace-liability-extension/extending-vat-online-marketplace-liability)).
- Scope: UK VAT-registered sellers, and others over £90k "separately on each platform"; goods only; B2B, personal and second-hand excluded [F]([factsheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/factsheet-on-extending-vat-online-marketplace-liability-rules/extending-vat-online-marketplace-liability-rules-factsheet)).
- Start: "October 2027", exact day by secondary legislation [F].
