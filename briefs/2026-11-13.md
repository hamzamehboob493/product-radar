### Card 1. Shopify usage-billing abuse guard · 65/100 · VALIDATE

**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Product marketing and SaaS growth
**What it is:** an SDK plus dashboard for Shopify app developers who bill by usage: per-store caps, holds on fresh or risky stores, alerts when a store burns usage and uninstalls, and a check of usage recorded against usage actually billed.
**Who pays:** indie and small-studio Shopify app developers (1 to 10 people) using usage or hybrid pricing, worldwide.
**The problem:** developers report "bad actors who create a fresh store" and leave after consuming usage (12 Jun), "Stores consuming usage without valid payment method is a big issue!" (26 Jun), and a merchant who triggers 200 events then uninstalls is billed about $1.63 while the developer pays real costs (25 May) [F] ([thread](https://community.shopify.dev/t/shopify-app-pricing-the-next-generation-of-app-billing/34221?page=3)). Shopify said it is "not exploring updating the Billing API" (23 Jun) [F]. The Partner API is "a money ledger" with no subscription state [F] ([Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/what-are-you-using-to-track-app-sales-mrr-now-that-mantle-is-shutting-down/652928)).
**Why now:** Mantle (billing and analytics for app makers) closed billing accounts 30 Sep with data destroyed [F] ([Mantle](https://docs.heymantle.com/wind-down)); Shopify announced annual plans with usage charges as "coming soon" on 11 Nov [F].
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: 17,891 active apps and 11,352 developers as of 25 May 2026 [R] ([AppJubilee](https://www.appjubilee.io/shopify-app-store-report-2026)); share on usage billing unknown
- Money flowing: Mantle charged by tracked revenue and served "hundreds of" app businesses [R] ([adsx](https://www.adsx.com/blog/mantle-shutting-down-shopify-app-partners))
- Competitors: Marmeto Elevate (free, analytics, no usage guard) [F]; Letsmetrix (free, rankings, "isn't a billing platform") [F]; Baremetrics (priced by MRR, no usage features shown) [F]; in-house scripts
- The gap: nobody sells usage-charge protection; developers build their own [F]
**Our angle:** the guard, not another MRR dashboard. Drop-in middleware for Remix/Node apps.
**Build difficulty:** Easy-medium · about 50 h, 1 engineer 6 days (AI-assisted, includes QA) · Node/TypeScript SDK, Next.js dashboard, Postgres, Redis · Shopify Admin API (app subscriptions, usage records), App Events API, uninstall webhooks, Partner API · hardest part: reconciling App Events with billed charges while that API is still changing · about 5 h/month upkeep
**Where to publish and sell:**
- Main: own site with Polar checkout (dev tool, no app-store review)
- Secondary: community.shopify.dev, Shopify Partners Slack and X circles, npm, Shopify App Founders newsletter, Show HN
- Payment: Polar 5% + 50¢ (+1.5% international); Pakistan payouts listed [F] ([Polar](https://polar.sh/docs/merchant-of-record/supported-countries))
**Pricing and economics (estimates):** $29/mo (1 app), $79/mo (3 apps), $149/mo studio. Running cost about $60/mo at 50 customers; margin about 85%. Break-even on build labor about 30 customer-months. 25 customers ≈ $1,125/mo; 100 ≈ $4,500/mo (at $45 average).
**Launch plan:** D0 page and free "usage leak" estimator · D1 to D5 validation · D5 go/no-go · D5 to D9 build SDK, rules and alerts · D10 launch on own site
**First 10 customers:** developers who posted in the App Pricing thread, Mantle refugees in community.shopify.dev, usage-billed apps found in the App Store. Pitch: "Stop paying for stores that burn your usage and uninstall. Caps, holds and alerts in one import."
**Validation test (before building):** landing page, free estimator (paste Partner API export, see unbilled usage), $29 founding pre-order on Polar, 20 messages to developers, 1 post in the dev community. 5 days, about $40. Go: 5 pre-orders or 3 design partners with usage billing. No-go: under 2 pre-orders and under 25 estimator runs.
**Risks:**
- Shopify adds payment-method status or pre-authorisation for usage charges (no mitigation; sell as interim)
- Developers keep DIY scripts (open-source the core, charge for alerts and reconciliation)
- App Events API changes (pin versions, contract tests)
**Confidence:** Low-medium. Pain verified in public posts; willingness to pay not.

- Market size: UK Shopify store count not checked this run [U]
- Money flowing: shipping-estimate apps charge $8 to $49/mo but have 0 to 3 reviews each [F] ([Shipping-Shock Preventer](https://apps.shopify.com/shipping-shock-preventer), [Calcurates](https://apps.shopify.com/calcurates-product-page-shipping))

- The gap: no listing mentions the CMA or shows a combined total on collection pages [F]
**Our angle:** the compliance-framed app, UK first, AU next.
**Build difficulty:** Easy-medium · about 45 h, 1 engineer 5 days · Shopify Remix app, theme app extension, delivery profiles API · hardest part: rates that vary by postcode or weight · about 4 h/month upkeep
**Where to publish and sell:**
- Main: Shopify App Store (review time and Pakistan payout path unconfirmed this run)

### D1. Track A: Shopify usage-billing abuse guard (65, VALIDATE)

**1. Idea and buyer.** Web SaaS plus npm SDK. Buyer: Shopify app developers (solo and small studios, global, many in US, EU, India and Pakistan) that charge usage or hybrid fees. Painful workflow: every month they absorb costs from stores that consume metered features and uninstall, or that have no valid payment method, and they reconcile usage by hand because the Partner API has no subscription state [F]. Who pays: the developer or studio owner. Why now: Mantle's billing product is gone (30 Sep) [F], Shopify App Pricing now carries usage billing through an App Events API that developers call unstable (3 Aug post) [F], and annual plans with usage are "coming soon" (11 Nov) [F]. Smallest promise: "No store burns more than $X of your usage before it has paid."

**2. Proven model and angle.** Mantle served "hundreds of" app businesses and charged by tracked revenue [R]; Baremetrics and ChartMogul prove developers pay for subscription analytics generally [I]. Customers still lack: payment-status transparency ("Stores consuming usage without valid payment method is a big issue!") and abuse protection (developers "have built their own protections") [F] ([thread](https://community.shopify.dev/t/shopify-app-pricing-the-next-generation-of-app-billing/34221?page=3)). Our angle: guard and reconciliation, not dashboards. Copy risk: high for Shopify itself, medium for Elevate or Letsmetrix (they are free analytics tools and would need to monetise).

**3. Evidence with numbers.** 17,891 active apps, 11,352 developers (25 May 2026) [R] ([AppJubilee](https://www.appjubilee.io/shopify-app-store-report-2026)). Share on usage billing [U]. Bottom-up [E]: if 5% of developers use usage billing, about 570 buyers; 5% of those paying is about 28 customers. The worked loss example from the thread: 200 events billed at about $1.63 [F]; developer cost per event [U].

**4. Competitors.**

| Product | Price | Strength | Weakness (evidence) | Our entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marmeto Elevate | Free to start [F] | Revenue, churn, at-risk flags | No usage guard mentioned [F] ([Marmeto](https://marmeto.com/blogs/elevate/best-shopify-saas-analytics-tools-2026-top-mantle-alternatives)) | Guard and caps |
| Letsmetrix | Free [F] | Rankings and reviews | "isn't a billing platform" [F] ([Letsmetrix](https://letsmetrix.com/heymantle-alternatives)) | Billing-side tool |
| Baremetrics | Priced by MRR [F] | Mature analytics | No usage-charge features shown [F] ([Baremetrics](https://baremetrics.com/compare/mantle-alternative)) | Shopify-specific usage |
| DIY scripts | Free | Tailored | Each developer rebuilds it [F] | Drop-in SDK |

**5. MVP scope and effort.** Capabilities: (1) SDK wrapper around usage-record creation with per-store caps and a fresh-store hold; (2) payment-status and uninstall webhooks that flag burn-and-leave stores; (3) nightly reconciliation of recorded vs billed usage; (4) Slack and email alerts; (5) simple dashboard. Hours (AI-assisted, includes review): SDK 12, webhooks and rules 10, reconciliation 10, dashboard 8, Polar and onboarding 4, QA 6 = 50 h. Deployment 2 h. Support about 3 h/week first month. Maintenance about 5 h/month.
