### 1. UK vet complaints log + CMA compliance tracker · 70/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (2 to 3 weeks build, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Clinic admin
**What it is:** web app for independent UK vet practices. It logs client complaints, runs the legal clocks, and tracks every CMA deadline.
**Who pays:** practice managers or owners at independent UK practices (1 to 14 sites).
**The problem:** the CMA order requires every vet business, within 6 months, to "acknowledge the complaint in writing within 5 working days", "send a full letter of response within 8 weeks", keep a complaints log and offer mediation. Smaller practices must publish price lists within 6 months and give written estimates for £500+ within 12 months. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/what-veterinary-businesses-and-vets-need-to-do-following-the-cmas-final-vets-report>
**Why now:** orders were due by 23 Sep 2026, so the complaints deadline lands around late March 2027. <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-concludes-market-investigation-with-major-reforms-to-veterinary-sector>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: about 3,560 UK vet businesses; corporates owned about 60% by 2023, so about 1,400 independents (estimate). <https://vetdrive.co/how-many-vets-uk/>
- Money: VetComply plans £29/mo per practice (waitlist, no customers yet) and quotes the CMA's estimated compliance cost of £450 to 550 a year. <https://vetcomply.co.uk/>
- Competitors:
  - Provet, which says it has "no complaints module"
  - VetComply, broad compliance, pre-launch
  - Agilio, which sells training and HR, not a log
  - Pricebook.vet, free price lists only
- Gap: nobody sells a live complaints log with statutory clocks yet.
**Our angle:** first to market with a complaints workflow that works alongside any practice software, plus a CMA checklist. Ship before VetComply launches.

### 2. Pipedrive bulk dedupe with merge undo · 70/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (2 weeks build, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Sales ops
**What it is:** app that finds fuzzy duplicate people, companies and deals in Pipedrive, bulk merges them, and lets you undo any merge.
**Who pays:** admins and RevOps at small to mid Pipedrive teams (5 to 50 users) after imports or tool migrations.
**The problem:** Pipedrive's native merge only matches exact names, can't bulk merge, and merges "can't be un-merged". <https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/merge-duplicates>
**Why now:** no trigger; steady, recurring pain.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: Pipedrive has 100,000+ paying companies. <https://www.pipedrive.com/en/blog/how-pipedrive-reached-100000-paying-customers>
- Money:
  - Dedupely on Pipedrive: 300+ installs, 4.5 stars from 122 reviews, $40/mo. <https://www.pipedrive.com/en/marketplace/app/dedupely/daeafd7d3dd9c2a2>
  - The same job on HubSpot (Koalify) has 5,000+ installs, and a review complains of paying "900-1.100€ a month for a deduping tool".
- Competitors:
  - Dedupely, $40/mo, solid; its merge docs never mention undo
  - Insycle, free to 5,000 records, broad and complex
  - Pipedrive native, exact match only
- Gap: safe, reversible bulk merging; fuzzy matching for non-Latin names (Arabic, Urdu); a cheap tier for small teams.
**Our angle:** "Dedupe without fear": every merge is snapshotted and can be undone in one click.

**Validation test:** landing page, plus posts in r/cursor and r/ClaudeAI. Go at 40 signups or 5 pre-orders in 7 days.
