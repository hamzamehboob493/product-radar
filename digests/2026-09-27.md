### 1. Companies House ID-check tracker · 67/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Legal and accounting (practice admin)
**What it is:** a UK accounting practice pastes its client company numbers and sees which directors and PSCs still have to verify their identity with Companies House, and when. The app emails reminders to those clients.
**Who pays:** UK accounting and bookkeeping practices with 50 to 1,000 client companies.
**The problem:** existing directors must verify "at the same time as they file their next annual confirmation statement, during a 12-month transition period" from 18 Nov 2025. About 6 to 7 million people are in scope. <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/companies-house-confirms-identity-verification-rollout-from-18-november-2025> Acting unverified becomes an offence. <https://changestoukcompanylaw.campaign.gov.uk/identity-verification/>
**Why now:** the transition ends about 18 Nov 2026. After that, every new director and PSC still has a 14-day window.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: ICAEW alone supervises about 10,000 firms <https://www.icaew.com/regulation/aml-supervision/our-role-as-an-aml-supervisor>
- Money: VerifyMyClient sells verification plus due and overdue alerts, "from £2 per verification" <https://verifymyclient.com/new-for-2026/>
- Competitors:
  - VerifyMyClient: per-verification pricing, with tracking bundled in
  - FigsFlow: quote only
  - Practice suites (TaxCalc, IRIS, Karbon): no ID-check tracker found (unknown)
  - A spreadsheet plus Companies House lookups, one company at a time
- Gap: a flat-price tracker for clients who verify themselves for free through GOV.UK One Login
**Our angle:** one flat monthly price for the whole client list, with no per-check fee.

### 2. EU parcel product-ID (PID) checker · 65/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Logistics and e-commerce
**What it is:** upload a product CSV from Shopify, Woo, Etsy or any other shop. The tool checks every item for the 3 product IDs EU customs require from 1 Nov, flags gaps, suggests HS codes and exports a file ready for a customs broker or 3PL.
**Who pays:** US, UK and Asian online brands shipping parcels worth €150 or less into the EU, with 50 to 5,000 products.
**The problem:** EU Regulation 2026/1022 makes three IDs per item mandatory from 1 Nov 2026 <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202601022>:
- the seller's SKU
- the supplier's part number
- the GTIN barcode, or exception code Y081 if there isn't one
The seller or its representative files the declaration. <https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en> Missing data means rejected declarations and held parcels. <https://passportglobal.com/blog/eu-product-identifiers-pid-guide-2026/> Shopify's product export has no supplier part-number column (Shopify Community, 22 Sep).
**Why now:** mandatory 1 Nov 2026, 35 days away, on top of the €3 per-line duty in place since 1 Jul.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: number of stores shipping to the EU is unknown
- Money in adjacent customs tools:
  - Zonos Duty & Tax: 159 reviews, $2,500 to $4,000/yr <https://apps.shopify.com/duty-and-tax-calculator-iglobal-stores>
  - Easyship: 403 reviews
  - DutyCode: $9/mo, 3 reviews <https://apps.shopify.com/dutycode>
- Competitors:
  - Shopify native fields: free, but no supplier part number
  - DutyCode and Tariff HS: HS codes only, no PID
  - Zonos Catalog: adding PIDs, but only on Zonos contracts

**What it is:** a complaints log for independent UK vet practices. It runs the legal clocks and letters for the new CMA rules and works alongside any practice software.
**Who pays:** practice managers at independent practices (1 to 14 sites).
**The problem:** under the CMA order (published 22 Sep 2026), practices must:
- acknowledge complaints within 5 working days
- respond in full within 8 weeks
- keep a log
- offer mediation
<https://www.accountantsforvets.co.uk/cma-remedies-guide>
**Why now:** the duties start about 22 Mar 2027 (estimate), for practices of every size.
**What changed since 25 Sep (score down from 70):**
- VDS insures "more than 95%" of UK vets <https://www.thevds.co.uk/news/theVDS/who-are-the-vds> and announced VetResolve on 27 Jul: a complaints dashboard with reminders and timelines, subscription, members only. <https://www.vetclick.com/news/vds-launches-vetresolve-to-help-practices-meet-new-complaints-handling-requirements-p12101.php>
- VetGuard sells "the complaints record and its clocks" at £49/mo + VAT. <https://vetguard.uk/>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: about 5,300 practices <https://vetcomply.co.uk/>; about 40% independent in 2023 <https://vetdrive.co/how-many-vets-uk/>
- Money: VetGuard at £49/mo, VetComply planned at £29/mo (still a waitlist), VetResolve price unknown
- Competitors:
  - VetResolve: not launched yet
  - VetGuard: bundled with price lists, no reviews found
  - Provet: complaint templates still in development
  - A free spreadsheet
- Gap: a cheap tool for practices outside VDS, or that find VetResolve too dear

### 4. EWS audit for IT providers (MSPs) · 56/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Security and IT
**What it is:** one report showing, across all of an MSP's client Microsoft 365 tenants, which apps still use Exchange Web Services (EWS). It also writes the allow-list script each tenant needs and tracks progress to April 2027.
**Who pays:** MSPs managing 10 to 200 Microsoft 365 tenants, in the US, UK and Australia.
**The problem:** Microsoft starts disabling EWS in October 2026 and switches it off completely in April 2027. <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/exchange/clients-and-mobile-in-exchange-online/deprecation-of-ews-exchange-online> Its usage report covers one tenant at a time, refreshes weekly and has no documented API. <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/admin/activity-reports/ews-usage> One MSP asked CIPP: "A CIPP report that can flag EWS usage across all managed tenants would materially reduce risk". CIPP closed the request as not planned. <https://github.com/KelvinTegelaar/CIPP/issues/5320>
**Why now:** tenants on default settings flip to disabled from 1 Oct <https://lazyadmin.nl/office-365/ews-is-being-blocked-in-exchange-online-how-to-find-every-app-still-using-it/>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: about 40,000 US MSPs <https://expertinsights.com/it-management/30-msp-stats>
- Money: MSPs pay $250/mo and up for multi-tenant tools <https://www.cloudcapsule.io/pricing>, <https://www.augmentt.com/pricing/>
- Competitors:
  - Microsoft's report: free, one tenant at a time
  - CIPP: free, declined the feature
  - Free single-tenant PowerShell scripts
  - Augmentt and Inforcer: no EWS feature found

### 5. Pipedrive duplicate merge with undo (returning) · 55/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Sales and revenue ops
**What it is:** fuzzy, bulk duplicate merging for Pipedrive, where every merge can be undone from a snapshot.
**Who pays:** Pipedrive admins at small sales teams (5 to 50 users), plus Pipedrive consultants.
**The problem:** Pipedrive's own merge works one pair at a time, and merged records "can't be un-merged". <https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/merge-duplicates> A Dedupely review on G2: "no way to apply common sense to the automatic merge logic". <https://www.g2.com/products/dedupely/reviews>
**Why now:** no trigger; steady demand.
**What changed since 25 Sep (score down from 70):** Insycle lets users "review the audit trail report and revert any changes", and it's free up to 5,000 records. <https://support.insycle.com/hc/en-us/articles/6584842792599-Frequently-Asked-Questions>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: Pipedrive has 100,000+ paying companies <https://www.pipedrive.com/en/blog/how-pipedrive-reached-100000-paying-customers>
- Money:
  - Dedupely: $40/mo, 300+ installs, 122 reviews
  - Insycle: $125/mo at 50,000 records
- Competitors:
  - Dedupely: merge logic is rigid, and no undo was found
  - Insycle: 4.0 stars from 5 reviews
  - Pipedrive native: one pair at a time
- Gap: undo that restores linked deals and notes (whether Insycle can do this is unknown)
