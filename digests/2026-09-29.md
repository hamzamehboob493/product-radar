### 1. Harassment evidence file · 66/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** HR
**What it is:** a UK pub, shop or clinic completes a guided risk assessment for harassment of staff by customers and contractors, logs incidents, records who read the policy, and exports a dated evidence file.
**Who pays:** owners and ops managers of UK customer-facing SMBs (10 to 250 staff), and HR consultants who serve them.
**The problem:** from 30 Oct employers are liable for third-party harassment. Acas expects risk assessments, incident monitoring, policies and training. <https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-rights-act-2025/harassment-law-changes> The "all reasonable steps" test is strict: one missed step can mean liability. <https://www.hilldickinson.com/our-view/articles/third-party-harassment-employees-gain-new-protection-from-october-2026/>
**Why now:** 30 Oct 2026, 31 days away. Claim time limits double to 6 months from 1 Oct. <https://www.blakemorgan.co.uk/employment-rights-act-2025-october-2026-implementation-dates-confirmed/>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: 176,685 UK hospitality businesses <https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10333/>
- Money: EmployerKit gap audit from £49 <https://employerkit.com/guides/third-party-harassment-employer-duty-2026>; small HR suites £22 to £24/mo (reported) <https://madyhr.ai/compare/hr-software-uk>
- Competitors: Breathe and BrightHR (no such feature listed); Culture Shift (enterprise, no price); free EHRC and Acas guides (no log)
- Gap: no cheap tool found combining assessment, log and sign-offs
**Our angle:** one job, one price, live before 30 Oct.
