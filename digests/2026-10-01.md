### 1. Synthetic-performer ad disclosure kit · 60/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Marketing and advertising
**What it is:** upload an ad with an AI-generated person or voice, get the NY and CA disclosure burned in (text, end card or audio line), and keep a dated register of AI assets and approvers.
**Who pays:** US-facing performance agencies, AI UGC studios and DTC brands, 2 to 50 staff.
**The problem:** New York's law has applied since 9 Jun 2026, $1,000 for a first violation and $5,000 after. <https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-01-29-new-york-enacts-synthetic-performer-disclosure-law-for-advertisements-including-those-using-generative-ai> California SB 1050 adds a disclosure duty from 1 Jan 2027 with no knowledge defence and private suits under unfair-competition law (reported). <https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102o1m6/fake-performer-more-penalties-californias-synthetic-performer-law-enters-the-c/>
**Why now:** SB 1050 signed 16 Sep 2026 (reported), in force 1 Jan 2027, 92 days away. <https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/09/state-quick-hits-california-privacy-and-ai>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: Meta reports 4M+ advertisers on its generative-AI ad tools; MakeUGC claims 10,000+ companies (reported Jul 2026) <https://trends.vc/synthetic-ugc-ads-ai-actors-managed-agencies-creative-fatigue/>
- Money: Creatify $9M ARR, HeyGen about $100M ARR, Arcads $16M seed (reported, same source)
- Competitors: platform AI labels (free; guides treat state law as separate) <https://prizmad.com/guides/ai-ad-disclosure-labels>; Intercepta scans (price unknown); generators (no disclosure feature seen); manual editing (free)
- Gap: nobody found who adds the prescribed wording and keeps an audit trail across generators

### 2. Envato exit kit · 59/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Design and creative
**What it is:** a hosted service for CodeCanyon authors of PHP, Laravel, Flutter and React Native items. Buyers enter their Envato purchase code, get a licence and update access from the author's own store, and new sales flow through Polar.
**Who pays:** non-WordPress CodeCanyon authors, solo or small studios, many in South and Southeast Asia and Eastern Europe.
**The problem:** since 1 Jul 2026 every author keeps 50%, exclusive authors used to keep up to 87.5%, and authors may now sell elsewhere. <https://author.envato.com/hub/changes-to-envato-market-revenue-share-and-exclusivity-what-you-need-to-know/> A $79 theme now pays the author about $33.50 instead of $58.62 (author's estimate). <https://purethemes.net/envato-author-exclusivity-merchant-of-record/>
**Why now:** no deadline; authors are moving after the first 50% payout on 15 Aug.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: CodeCanyon author and item counts unknown
- Money: Freemius takes about 10% <https://freemius.com/envato-alternative/>; ChargePanda sells an Envato claim module for $19 one-time on its self-hosted platform <https://www.chargepanda.com/products/envato-integration>
- Competitors: Freemius (migration guide "for WordPress only") <https://freemius.com/help/documentation/migration/migrating-from-codecanyon-to-freemius/>; ChargePanda (self-hosted, one-time products only); Polar licence keys (no Envato claim); abandoned CodeCanyon verifiers (EPCV $9, 9 sales) <https://codecanyon.net/item/epcv-envato-purchase-code-verifier/19590094>
- Gap: no hosted claim-plus-update service for non-WordPress items

### 3. Atlassian step-cost analyser · 55/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Developer tools
**What it is:** an admin tool that reads Jira and Confluence automation rules, estimates the steps each rule burns per month (including runs that match nothing), ranks the costliest rules and suggests cheaper structures.
**Who pays:** Jira admins and Atlassian consultancies at companies on Standard, Premium and Enterprise Cloud.
**The problem:** from 3 Dec 2026 every executed trigger, condition, action, branch and loop is a billable step, runs ending NO_MATCH included, overage $0.50 per 1,000 steps, extra usage on by default. <https://support.atlassian.com/cloud-automation/docs/how-is-my-usage-calculated/> Atlassian's usage page is per app, not per rule; feature request AUTO-1699 is open. <https://community.atlassian.com/forums/discussion/3285685/automation-step-allowance-do-automations-stop-before-extra-usage-billing-starts>
**Why now:** billing starts 3 Dec 2026, 63 days away.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: every Atlassian Cloud customer; count not checked
- Money: no paid step analyser found; consultancies sell audits (prices unknown)
- Competitors: Atlassian Platform usage page (free, per app); ReleaseTEAM Step Cost Calculator (free, manual run counts) <https://steps.releaseteam.com/>; consultancies
- Gap: per-rule view with real run counts
