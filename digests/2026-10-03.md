### 3. UAE supplier-check file for input VAT · 65/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 3)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** GCC and Arabic-first
**What it is:** an English and Arabic web app. Import the payables ledger, and it sorts suppliers into the Decision 13 tiers by rolling 12-month spend. It then tracks each required check with evidence uploads, re-checks yearly, logs red flags and exports an audit pack. Records only, no tax advice.
**Who pays:** UAE VAT-registered SMEs (finance manager) and the UAE accounting firms that run their books.
**The problem:** FTA Decision 13 of 2026, in force 1 Oct 2026: input VAT is denied unless suppliers and supplies are verified and documented. Suppliers over AED 375,000 a year need bank confirmation and public-source checks, re-done at least every 12 months, and a written policy is required. <https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/09/09/uae-fta-mandates-supplier-due-diligence-for-input-vat-recovery/> <https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1832-uae-issues-decision-introducing-due-diligence-requirements-for-input-vat-recovery>
**Why now:** in force since 1 Oct 2026; the first VAT returns that rely on it are being prepared now.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: number of UAE VAT registrants unknown (not verified this run)
- Money already flowing: advisers sell policy drafting and spend analysis for this rule (no prices shown) <https://www.aaccounting.me/insights/fta-decision-13-supplier-verification>; Alvarez & Marsal says it needs vendor-master fields and cumulative spend monitoring <https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/thought-leadership/middle-east-tax-alert-uae-fta-decision-no-13-of-2026-supplier-and-supply-verification-before-input-tax-deduction>
