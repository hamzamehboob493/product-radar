### 1. Upwork proposal copilot · 62/100 · WATCHLIST (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** HR, recruiting and freelancing
**What it is:** A Chrome extension that scores the Upwork job you are viewing and drafts a proposal you edit and send yourself. No job feed, no background requests, no auto-send.
**Who pays:** Upwork agencies (2 to 20 people) and full-time freelancers, mainly US, UK, EU, AU.
**The problem:** Upwork bans extensions that act "faster or more frequently than a human", up to a permanent block [F] <https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/43342677368467>. The best-selling rivals lead with auto-bidding (GigRadar sends bids "24/7") [F] <https://gigradar.io/>.
**Why now:** No dated trigger. Upwork's fee rose to up to 15% in 2026 [R] <https://www.sirapays.com/blog/upwork-2026-fee-hike-freelancer-diversification>
**Market analysis:**
- Lancer.app: $19,194 MRR, 118 subscriptions [F] <https://trustmrr.com/startup/lancer-app>
- U Never Sleep: $2,182 MRR, $15/month [F] <https://trustmrr.com/startup/u-never-sleep>
- Competitors:
  - Lancer: $99 to $333/month, auto-apply.
  - Vollna: $24 to $62/month manual, $59 to $640/month auto-bid [F] <https://www.vollna.com/pricing>
  - Upwork Uma: free tier, no scorer found [F].
- Gap: a policy-safe tool for agencies afraid of bans. No direct buyer complaints collected yet [U].
**Our angle:** "Bid less, win more, keep your account." On-page only, with an agency case-study library.

**Market analysis:**
- Classic Expenses user count: unknown.
- Money already flowing:
  - Dext: 1,116 reviews, £24.17/month for 5 users [F] <https://dext.com/uk/business/pricing>
  - ApprovalMax: 658 reviews. Lightyear claims 70,000+ users [F] <https://apps.xero.com/us/collection/top-bills-and-expense-management-software-2026>
- Competitors:
  - Xero Expenses: ~£2.50/user, Grow plan and up [R].
  - Dext: priced by document volume.
  - Expensify: per-user, US-centric.
- Gap: flat price and simplicity. Evidence is moderate.
**Our angle:** Flat price, one-screen approval, import of Classic claims history.
