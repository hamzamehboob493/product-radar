### 1. Shopify script-tag sunset checker · 67/100 · VALIDATE (1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** E-commerce
**What it is:** Paste a store URL, see which scripts still load through script tags, which app added each, and whether that app has an app-embed replacement. Agencies get bulk reports for client stores, re-checked weekly until the cutoff.
**Who pays:** Shopify agencies and freelancers (5 to 200 client stores), then merchants with many apps. US, UK, EU, AU.
**The problem:** Script tags stop running on storefronts on 1 Mar 2027 and fail silently [F] <https://shopify.dev/changelog/online-store-script-tags-deprecation>. No admin page lists which apps use them, and on 27 Sep Shopify staff said the planned merchant warning is not shipping [F] <https://community.shopify.dev/t/deprecation-script-tags-will-stop-running-on-march-1-2027/37108>
**Why now:** Hard cutoff 1 Mar 2027; BFCM freezes store changes mid-Nov to early Dec, so Dec to Feb is the panic window.
**Market analysis:**
- Stores and apps affected: unknown ("thousands of apps" is an agency claim [F] <https://www.ontapgroup.com/blog/shopify-script-tags-die-in-6-months>).
- Money flowing: five code-cleaner apps at $4.99 to $49/month, all launched 2026, all 0 reviews [F]; agencies sell manual audits.
- Competitors: AppAudit (free to $49/mo agency, reads script tags) <https://apps.shopify.com/appaudit>; Script Scan (free to $14.99) <https://apps.shopify.com/script-scan>; ThemeSweep ($9.99 to $39.99) <https://apps.shopify.com/themesweep>. None mentions the deadline.
- Gap: a merchant-facing list of what breaks, with fixes.
**Our angle:** The only tool built around the cutoff, free without installing, mapping each tag to its replacement.

**Why now:** Regulations published 9 Sep 2026 [R]; first window opens 15 Dec.
**Market analysis:**
- Agencies and properties per region: unknown.
- Money flowing: agencies already buy compliance trackers (Bilans for Reapit, price unknown) [F] <https://thenegotiator.co.uk/news/new-reapit-compliance-tool-to-track-rra-readiness/>
- Competitors: Bilans (Reapit only, no database feature mentioned); August (free to 2 tenancies, landlord side) [F]; Reapit, Alto, Goodlord (no database feature announced).
- Gap: no bulk path, so agencies need a chase-and-pack workflow.
**Our angle:** CRM-neutral, database-specific, ahead of agent guidance.
