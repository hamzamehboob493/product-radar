### 1. Smartlook exit kit · 61/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** SaaS growth
**What it is:** Paste a Smartlook API key. We archive events, funnels and session metadata to CSV or your own database, then rebuild your events and funnels in PostHog or Mixpanel.
**Who pays:** product and growth teams at SMBs and agencies still on Smartlook; US, UK, AU, EU.
**The problem:** Cisco ends support 31 Aug 2027 <https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/software/smartlook-com-eol.html>. All data is deleted 30 Sep 2027 and "Splunk will not be migrating any historical data" <https://www.koji.so/blog/smartlook-alternatives-2026>. Built-in CSV export is capped at 1,000 users per segment; replay export unconfirmed <https://www.uxwizz.com/blog/smartlook-shutting-down>
**Why now:** hard deletion date; no migration tool exists today.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: 19,202 sites still detected, 11,511 matched companies (US 2,097, UK 671, AU 379) on 29 Sep 2026 <https://technologychecker.io/technology/smartlook>. Paying share unknown (free plan exists).
- Money flowing: replacements sell to switchers: Mouseflow from 25/mo, UXWizz from $299, Koji EUR 29 to 79/mo
- Competitors: Microsoft Clarity (free, no history), FullStory (free tier), Splunk DXA (quote, no history), DIY API (dev time)
- The gap: nobody sells the exit itself; both recent roundups say users must export through the API themselves
**Our angle:** a neutral exit tool that keeps history and works with any replacement.

### 2. UK car-finance redress evidence pack · 59/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Automotive
**What it is:** Dealers import each lender's request batch, match agreements to old DMS exports and scanned deal files, log missing or destroyed records, and send a lender-ready pack with an audit trail. No claim assessment, no legal advice.
**Who pays:** UK independent and small franchised used-car dealers and finance brokers, 1 to 20 rooftops.
**The problem:** FCA redress scheme covers about 12.1M agreements (2007 to 2024), about £10bn. Lenders request records "in bulk", for example 500 at once with separate deadlines, and want decision-ready data, audit trails and a destruction log <https://www.restore.co.uk/informationmanagement/resource-hub/insights/how-dealers-should-prepare-for-motor-finance-redress-delivery/>
**Why now:** batches are arriving now; scheme partly paused pending an Upper Tribunal decision <https://www.forvismazars.com/uk/en/insights/financial-services-insights/motor-finance-consumer-redress-for-dealers>
**Market analysis:**
- Size: number of dealers receiving requests unknown; NFDA calls data readiness a top priority <https://nfda-uk.co.uk/nfda-update-documentation-data-readiness-remains-a-top-priority-for-members/>
- Money flowing: Restore runs a managed motor-finance service (price unknown)
- Competitors: Restore (managed, quote-based), NFDA Business Solutions (unknown), DMS vendors (bundled, recent data only), spreadsheets (no audit trail)
- The gap: no self-serve, flat-price tool for small dealers
**Our angle:** software a small dealer can run without hiring a scanning bureau.

### 5. FCC ID bulk listing validator · 53/100 · WATCHLIST
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Compliance / e-commerce
**What it is:** Upload a listing CSV; each radio device's FCC ID is checked against the FCC equipment database for a valid grant and matching product, with a fix list.
**Who pays:** high-volume electronics sellers on Amazon, eBay and Walmart; US.
**The problem:** Under FCC 26-50, marketplaces that hold goods must show a valid FCC ID from 1 Mar 2027; others must show it, verify it and collect seller certification from 1 Jun 2027 <https://icertifi.com/fcc-26-50-october-13-logic-bearing-components/>
**Why now:** rule effective 13 Oct 2026; deadlines 5 to 8 months out.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: number of affected sellers unknown
- Money flowing: none verified for this exact job
- Competitors: free single lookups (fccid.io-type sites), marketplace checks
- The gap: bulk, catalogue-level checking before listings get suppressed
**Our angle:** fix the whole catalogue once, before the marketplace asks.
**Build difficulty:** Easy · 4 engineer-days · Python, Postgres, FCC EAS data · hardest: matching product names to grants · 3 h/month.
**Where to publish and sell:** own site with Polar; Amazon seller forums and r/FulfillmentByAmazon.
**Pricing and economics (estimates):** $49 per 1,000 listings, $29/month monitoring.
**Launch plan:** D1 data pipeline · D3 checker and landing page · D5 to D9 validation · D10 launch.
**First 10 customers:** electronics sellers in seller communities. Pitch: "Check every FCC ID before Amazon pulls the listing."
**Validation test:** free 50-listing check, 7 days, $0. Go: 30 uploads and 5 paid. No-go: under 10 uploads.
**Risks:** marketplaces verify for free (no mitigation); sellers wait for enforcement.
**Confidence:** Low.
