### 1. FileCabinet CS self-serve exporter · 68/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Legal and accounting (practice admin)
**What it is:** A Windows tool a firm runs on its own server. It exports every FileCabinet CS drawer to SharePoint, Drive, Dropbox or a folder, keeps the client and folder tree, writes a metadata CSV and a report proving every document moved.
**Who pays:** US accounting and tax firms, 1 to 30 staff, on Thomson Reuters CS Professional Suite.
**The problem:** FileCabinet CS ends 31 Dec 2027. TR's free tool exports only to Onvio. Files sit in a legacy OLE format with "no bulk export UI"; batches halt and drawers get skipped <https://www.liscio.me/resources/how-to-export-files-from-filecabinet-cs>. Firms are told to start by May 2027 <https://www.myfirm360.com/blog/filecabinet-cs-alternatives/>.
**Why now:** EOL confirmed <https://www.smartvault.com/solutions/why-smartvault/compare/filecabinet-cs/>. Tax season blocks Jan to Apr, so Oct to Dec 2026 is a migration window.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: 10,000+ firms affected (Liscio claim, unverified) <https://liscio.me/resources/filecabinet-cs-is-end-of-life-your-migration-guide>
- Money flowing: Liscio migration $2,000 / $4,000 / $7,500+; DIY $8k to $15k; Onvio route $1.4k to $5k of staff time (same link). SmartVault says TR quoted about $18k.
- Competitors: TR tool (free, Onvio only); Liscio (from $2,000, ties credit to Liscio); SmartVault ($65/user/mo, SmartVault only); Canopy, TaxDome, Firm360 (only to their platform).
- The gap: no neutral, self-serve exporter to any destination.
**Our angle:** 4x to 10x cheaper, any destination, data never leaves the firm's machine.

### 2. Verizon Connect Work exit archive and converter · 58/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Home services
**What it is:** A browser tool (files stay on the PC) that turns Work's CSV exports and monthly photo, PDF and signature ZIPs into a searchable offline job archive, plus Jobber and Housecall Pro import files.
**Who pays:** Owners and office managers of small field-service firms in the US, UK, AU and NZ leaving Work.
**The problem:** No API; exports come in 2-year chunks and can take a week; Verizon will not hand data to other providers, and non-partners get no export schemas <https://support.work.fleetmatics.com/hc/en-us/articles/51912146755347-Comprehensive-FAQ>. International customers get no migration help <https://support.work.fleetmatics.com/hc/en-us/articles/52853107607699-Frequently-Asked-Questions-International-Customers>.
**Why now:** Daily operations end 31 Dec 2026; data purged 1 Apr 2027.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: customer count unknown.
- Money flowing: GoCanvas sells a Work migration suite "at the same price point" <https://explore.gocanvas.com/verizon-gocanvas/>.
- Competitors: GoCanvas and Service Fusion (partners, North America only, move you to them); Jobber/HCP free imports (no Work schema, no attachments) <https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/import-jobs/>.
- The gap: no archive viewer and no neutral converter.
**Our angle:** Keep every job photo and signature searchable forever, and move to any tool.

**Risks:** ControlC already loved (compete on price per org). Xero API cost (check tiers first).
**Confidence:** Low-medium.

**Pricing and economics (estimates):** Hosted margin about 75%; conversion unknown.
