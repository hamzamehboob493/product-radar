### 1. Power BI Q&A replacement for Pro-only tenants · 62/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Productivity / BI
**What it is:** a free scanner that finds every Q&A visual and synonym in your reports, plus a paid Power BI visual that answers plain-English questions using your own LLM key. Pro-only teams keep "ask a question" after Microsoft removes Q&A.
**Who pays:** organisations on Power BI Pro or PPU without Fabric capacity (US, UK, EU, AU), and their BI consultants.
**The problem:** Q&A visuals "stop working and are removed" by the end of December 2026 <https://m365admin.handsontek.net/retirement-power-bi-qa/>. Microsoft Learn now says February 2027 <https://learn.microsoft.com/power-bi/consumer/end-user-q-and-a>. Copilot is not in PPU and needs F2 capacity or higher <https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Service/Premium-Per-User-Copilot-Feature/m-p/4844355>.
**Why now:** retirement is 2.5 to 4 months away. Microsoft is "reviewing the approach for Pro licenses" <https://sumproduct.com/blog/power-bi-blog-no-more-qa/>.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of Pro-only tenants using Q&A unknown
- Money flowing: F2 costs about $155/month reserved to $263 pay-as-you-go <https://datatako.com/tools/fabric-capacity-calculator>
- Competitors: F2 + Copilot ($155 to $263/mo, workspace must move to capacity); Creative Data LLM Chatbot and PBI AI Agent (no price or ratings shown, cannot be certified) <https://appsource.microsoft.com/en-us/product/web-apps/pbi-ai-agent.pbi-ai-agent>; a manual rebuild (free, but no natural-language questions)
- The gap: nothing migrates Q&A synonyms or targets Pro-only tenants
**Our angle:** "Q&A for Pro". Keep your synonyms, at well under the cost of F2.

### 2. UK freelancer engagement evidence file · 57/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build · **Sector:** HR and freelancing
**What it is:** a dated file for each contractor recording substitution, control and pay facts with evidence, plus the proposed written statement of key terms. It records facts and never gives a status verdict.
**Who pays:** UK SMEs and agencies that use freelancers (construction, care, creative, IT), and their accountants.
**The problem:** the 14 Oct consultation proposes that the engager must prove self-employment, with an extra £100 to £20,000 per claim for repeat misclassification <https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/employment/2026-posts/uk-employment-status-consultation>. HMRC's free CEST tool keeps no evidence.
**Why now:** the consultation closes 8 Jan 2027 and has no start date yet <https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/makework-pay-employment-status-reform-and-protections-for-the-self-employed>. Right-to-work checks on individual subcontractors already apply from 1 Oct 2026.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: 4.57m UK self-employed (ONS, third-party figure); number of firms engaging them unknown
- Money flowing: Qdos charges £49+VAT per assessment and £125 for a full review, with 2,800+ engagers <https://www.goqdos.com/ir35/ir35-contract-assessment>
- Competitors: Qdos (verdicts, IR35, larger firms); Kingsbridge (demo-only pricing); Worksome (priced on freelancer spend); CEST (free, no records)
- The gap: an SME evidence record for employment status, not IR35 tax status
**Our angle:** "presumption-ready" files at £19/month, merged with the right-to-work locker (Watchlist 63).

**Market analysis:** Elevar from $225/mo (ChatGPT single-item orders only); Arcett $29/$79 and Shelfglance $19 (attribution only, 0 reviews); native toggles (free, store-wide).
**Our angle:** per-product exposure control first.
**Build difficulty:** Medium · 12 engineer-days · Remix, Admin GraphQL publications, GA4, Meta CAPI · hardest: Shopify may re-publish products
**Where to publish and sell:** Shopify App Store (review time and Pakistan payouts unconfirmed); Polar on own site as fallback
**Pricing and economics (estimates):** $19/month for rules, $49 with backfill; margin about 80%; 100 stores about $3,600/month.
**Validation test:** 1-day dev-store unpublish test, then 20 DMs to agencies, 7 days, under $50. Go: unpublish sticks and 3 merchants commit.
**Risks:** Shopify adds per-product controls · crowded attribution side.
**Confidence:** Low-medium.

**Where to publish and sell:** own site with Polar; HR consultants; HR platform marketplaces later
**Pricing and economics (estimates):** $79/month per employer, $299 consultant pack; margin about 90%; 25 customers about $2,000/month.

### 5. California AB 1159 edtech AI-training attestation pack · 52/100 · WATCHLIST
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Education
**What it is:** a guided form that maps student-data fields to every processor and AI model, then exports a dated "no AI training on student data" pack for school districts.
**Who pays:** small K-12 edtech vendors selling to California districts.
**The problem:** from 1 Jan 2027, K-12 operators must not use student data to train AI; families can sue for $500 per plaintiff per violation after a 60-day cure period <https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/california-enacts-over-two-dozen-key-privacy-and-ai-bills-into-law-in-2026-legislative-session.html>.
**Why now:** 2.5 months to go; higher-ed rules follow on 1 Jul 2027.
**Market analysis:** 15,900 apps and 233,924 active DPAs on the SDPC registry <https://privacy.a4l.org/>; iKeepSafe certification (price unpublished, no AI scope); free national DPA. Number of California vendors unknown.
**Our angle:** the cheapest district-ready AI-training evidence, updated whenever a model or processor changes.
**Build difficulty:** Easy · 5 engineer-days · Next.js, Postgres, PDF · 2 h/month
**Where to publish and sell:** own site with Polar; vendors listed on the SDPC registry
**Pricing and economics (estimates):** $199/year, $499 multi-product; margin about 92%; break-even on labour about 5 customers.
**Launch plan:** D1 landing and pre-order · D2 to D6 build · D7 QA · D10 launch.
**Validation test:** landing page plus 30 targeted emails to small vendors, 7 days, under $50. Go: 5 demo requests and 2 pre-orders. No-go: under 2 replies.
**Risks:** the law bans conduct and needs no document · districts may rely on the free DPA.
**Confidence:** Low.
