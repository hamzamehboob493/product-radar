### 1. Gmail Simple MAPI bridge (Affixa replacement) · 66/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Productivity / IT
**What it is:** a Windows add-on that makes the "Send by email" button in desktop software (Office, PDF printers, accounting apps, Explorer "Send to") open or send a Gmail message with the file attached.
**Who pays:** offices on Google Workspace with 5 to 200 PCs (US, UK, EU, AU), and the MSPs that run them.
**The problem:** Affixa, which has done this job for 17 years, is closing. Customers under 50 users cannot renew from 1 Nov 2026, all services stop 31 Jan 2027, and the Workspace integration is removed 1 Feb 2027. Affixa's own advice is a free open-source tool, Thunderbird, or buying its $500 DLL to build your own <https://help.affixa.com/article/100-sunsetting-and-retirement-of-affixa>
**Why now:** the 1 Nov renewal cutoff, then the 31 Jan 2027 shutdown (same link).
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: Affixa Enterprise shows 640K+ installs in the Workspace Marketplace <https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/affixa_enterprise_for_google_workspace/773666907438?hl=da>. Paying customers: unknown
- Money already flowing: Affixa sold paid seats for 17 years (seat price unknown) plus a $500 DLL licence
- Competitors: go-mapi (free, open source; no licence, maintainer or update date shown <https://alternativeto.net/software/affixa>); Thunderbird (free; moves users off Gmail); GmailDefaultMaker (free; no attachments); TVHGoogleMapi (discontinued)
- The gap: no supported, signed, MSP-deployable option is left
**Our angle:** the paid, supported version: silent install on every PC, a migration guide from Affixa, and $3 a user.

### 2. Apple 1.2 safety kit for AI content apps · 65/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Developer tools
**What it is:** a drop-in kit for small AI image, video and chat apps: report and block buttons (Swift and React Native), a review queue with response-time log, and prompt and output filters on cheap classifiers.
**Who pays:** indie developers and small studios with AI content apps on the App Store, worldwide.
**The problem:** Apple's guideline 1.2 now names "AI content creation apps" next to UGC platforms. They must have filtering, reporting "and timely responses", user blocking and published contact details. Apps that do not fix violations after notice can be removed <https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/>
**Why now:** revision published 16 Oct 2026 with no transition period <https://ppc.land/apples-app-store-rules-now-require-apps-to-remove-ai-intimate-imagery/>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of AI content apps affected is unknown
- Money already flowing: developers pay for classifiers, e.g. Sightengine $29/month for 10k operations, $99 for 40k <https://sightengine.com/pricing>
- Competitors: Sightengine (classifier only); Hive (price not verified); OpenAI moderation (flags only, no report or block); Stream (moderation inside its chat SDK, price not verified)
- The gap: the report, block and review workflow Apple asks for is left to each developer. A full competitor sweep was not finished this run, so check first
**Our angle:** "Pass 1.2 in a day": the whole compliance workflow, not just a classifier.

### 3. California SB 464 job-title to SOC mapper · 64/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** HR
**What it is:** upload your job titles, get each one mapped to the 23 SOC job groups California's pay data report now uses, with a reason, a review queue and an export.
**Who pays:** California employers with 100 to 1,000 staff, and HR consultants who file for clients.
**The problem:** SB 464 replaces the 10 EEO-1 categories with 23 SOC categories from the 2026 report, due May 2027. Penalties on CRD request are mandatory: up to $100 per employee, then $200 <https://www.seyfarth.com/news-insights/california-amends-its-pay-data-reporting-requirements.html>
**Why now:** first filing under the new groups in May 2027; mapping happens in Q1 2027 <https://www.berkshireassociates.com/blog/what-employers-need-to-know-about-soc-codes-in-2027>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: filers are employers with 100+ employees or 100+ contractor workers; count unknown
- Money already flowing: Berkshire sells mapping consulting; Trusaic sells a reporting platform <https://trusaic.com/regulatory-pay-transparency-reporting/california/>. Prices not published
- Competitors: Berkshire (consulting); Trusaic (platform); O*NET OnLine (free, one title at a time <https://www.onetonline.org/help/job_coding>); HRIS vendors (not confirmed)
- The gap: no self-serve bulk mapper with a public price was found
**Our angle:** $149 per filing instead of a consultant or platform.

### 4. Carrier DIM box auditor · 64/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Logistics
**What it is:** upload your box list and 90 days of orders; see which boxes jumped in billable weight under the new USPS rules and UPS/FedEx thresholds, the dollar impact, and which box to switch to.
**Who pays:** small US Shopify, WooCommerce and eBay sellers shipping bulky or light items.
**The problem:** since 12 Jul 2026 USPS uses a DIM factor of 139 above 1,728 cubic inches, rounds every dimension up to the whole inch, and flattened the Ground Advantage ounce tiers <https://about.usps.com/postal-bulletin/2026/pb22705/html/updt_001.htm>. eBay sellers call it "phantom weight" (8 lb billed as 16 lb) <https://community.ebay.com/forum/shipping-57923/topic/heads-up-usps-rounding-up-hidden-rate-hike-468569/>
**Why now:** UPS and FedEx peak surcharges run 25 Oct 2026 to mid-Jan 2027 <https://shipperhq.com/blog/carrier-rule-changes-hitting-your-shipping-and-how-to-get-ahead-of-them>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: unknown; a bottom-up count of affected shops was not possible
- Money already flowing: BoxPack IQ charges $9.99 to $29.99/month for box packing on Shopify <https://apps.shopify.com/boxpack-iq>; 3PLs sell DIM optimisation
- Competitors: BoxPack IQ (no DIM or USPS-139 features listed, 0 reviews); DCL SelectShip (3PL only); free one-box DIM calculators (no catalog audit); ShipperHQ and Paccurate (prices not verified)
- The gap: no catalog-wide old-vs-new audit for small sellers found
**Our angle:** a $29 one-off audit with box swap advice, no subscription.

### 5. Returning: Companies House ID-verification tracker · 63/100 · WATCHLIST (was Validate 67) (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Compliance
**What it is:** paste a practice's client company numbers; see every director and PSC still unverified, their due dates, and get alerts after the deadline.
**Who pays:** small UK accounting practices and company secretaries.
**The problem:** 6 to 7 million people still had to verify at end June (55% of directors, 42% of PSCs done); Companies House moves to enforcement after the deadline <https://www.lawdebenture.com/news-insights/millions-of-directors-still-unverified-the-17-november-deadline-is-closer-than-it-looks>
**Why now:** the transition closes 17 Nov 2026. What changed since 27 Sep: the officers API now shows verification details <https://forum.companieshouse.gov.uk/t/parsing-identity-verification-details-eccta-the-two-shapes-it-comes-in/12897>, and Inform Direct already shows portfolio status <https://brightsg.com/blog/inform-direct-the-home-of-eccta-compliance/>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: 6 to 7 million individuals in scope (same Law Debenture link); practice count not checked
- Money already flowing: practice suites (Bright) sell verification views; verification providers charge per check
- Competitors: Inform Direct (demo only, platform users only); BrightManager (manual status); ComplianceRadar on Apify (2 users, deprecated)
- The gap: a cheap, standalone portfolio view for firms not on Bright
**Our angle:** £19/month, works from a pasted list, no platform switch.
