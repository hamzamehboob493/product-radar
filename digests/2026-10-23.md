### 1. Returning: UK subscription notice and evidence engine · 59/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Compliance
**What it is:** connects to Stripe Billing, Chargebee or Paddle and sends every UK reminder notice on time, tracks the extra cooling-off windows, and keeps a proof file per customer.
**Who pays:** subscription SaaS, apps, memberships and boxes with UK consumers (UK, US, EU, AU sellers), 100 to 20,000 subscribers.
**The problem:** reminders before renewals and before the first full-price payment after a trial; a second 14-day cooling-off after trials and 12-month renewals; end-of-contract notice within 24 hours; an online exit ("email alone" does not count). The trader stays responsible even if a platform takes payment <https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6acf97da2de3184ccf8b814e/subscription-contracts-rules-guidance.pdf>
**Why now:** draft rules published 22 Oct 2026, apply from 1 Jan 2027, no grace period <https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/new-uk-consumer-subscription-rules-confirmed-for-january-2027-what-businesses-need-to-do-now/>. Was 51 on 27 Sep.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of UK-facing subscription sellers unknown
- Money already flowing: fines up to 10% of turnover reported <https://churnkey.co/guides/dmcca-subscription-rules>; no paid tool with traction found
- Competitors: Subnotice (Shopify only, free tier, 0 reviews <https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1104648/Subnotice>); billing tools' renewal emails (no cooling-off or proof log found); Churnkey (cancel flows, price unknown)
- The gap: nothing found for Stripe, Chargebee or Paddle sellers
**Our angle:** platform-neutral, with the cooling-off clock and proof file as the core.
