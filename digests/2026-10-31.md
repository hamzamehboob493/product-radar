### 1. Australian ADM disclosure kit · 58/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Compliance
**What it is:** list the software that makes or shapes decisions about people, send each vendor a one-link questionnaire, get a categorised draft of the new privacy-policy section and an evidence pack for the reviewer. Returning (59 on 3 Oct).
**Who pays:** Australian businesses covered by the Privacy Act (generally over A$3m turnover, plus health providers), and the privacy consultants, MSPs and accountants who serve them.
**The problem:** from 10 Dec privacy policies must describe the personal information automated programs use and the decisions they make or substantially shape. The duty sits with the business, not the vendor; vendors are slow to answer; many wrongly think human sign-off takes them out of scope. Penalties up to A$330k for administrative failures <https://allitservices.com.au/australias-new-automated-decision-making-privacy-rules-start-10-december-what-businesses-using-ai-must-do-now/>
**Why now:** OAIC APP 1 Guidelines v2 out 30 Sep; obligation starts 10 Dec 2026; categories are acceptable <https://www.allens.com.au/insights-news/insights/2026/10/mandatory-automated-decision-making-disclosure-requirements-are-coming-key-takeaways-from-the-oaic-guidance/>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of covered businesses unknown
- Money flowing: policy generators and consultants are paid; prices and ADM coverage unchecked (unknown)
- Competitors: law firms (hourly); Termly, iubenda, Clym (ADM section unknown); OAIC fact sheet (free, guidance only); spreadsheets
- The gap: the vendor-chasing and categorising step, which commentary names as the bottleneck
**Our angle:** inventory plus vendor questionnaire in one place, output ready for a reviewer; never a legal opinion.

### 2. Multi-state lease page-1 fee checker · 55/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Real estate
**What it is:** a landlord uploads a lease and fee list; the tool flags fees missing from page 1 and capped or banned fees for Illinois and six other states, and builds the page-1 fee summary. Returning idea (was 50 on 11 Oct).
**Who pays:** small US landlords and property managers using their own leases (Illinois owner-occupied 6 units or fewer are exempt).
**The problem:** in Illinois a mandatory fee not on lease page 1 is not owed; application fees capped at $50; renewal, walkthrough and after-hours fees banned; tenants can sue <https://www.illinoislegalaid.org/legal-information/rental-junk-fee-ban>. Landlords are confused about scope (BiggerPockets thread) <https://www.biggerpockets.com/forums/517/topics/1238937-illinois-house-passes-bill-banning-move-in-fees-for-renters>
**Why now:** Public Act 104-0479, delayed to 1 Jan 2027 by HB5234 <https://ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/View/104-0479>; similar page-1 rules in MN, VA, MA, CT, NV, CO <https://www.apartments.com/rental-manager/resources/leasing-and-laws/price-transparency-what-state-laws-mean-your-rental>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: Illinois landlord count unknown; channels: IRPOA, Chicagoland and Illinois Apartment Associations
- Money flowing: no paid lease-side tool verified (unknown)
- Competitors: Zillow Costs & Fees (free, listing only) <https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/pricing-transparency>; Apartments.com all-in pricing (free, listing only); attorney review; AppFolio, Buildium, TurboTenant (unchecked)
- The gap: nobody verified checks lease page 1 across states
**Our angle:** lease-side, multi-state, $29 per pack, no legal drafting.

### 3. WhatsApp applicant screener for hourly hiring · 53/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** HR and recruiting
**What it is:** one WhatsApp link in a job ad; applicants answer 5 questions (availability, location, right to work yes/no, experience, voice note) in English or Arabic; the employer gets a ranked shortlist and booked interview slots.
**Who pays:** independent restaurants, cafes, shops and cleaning firms with 5 to 100 staff in the UK, UAE and Saudi Arabia.
**The problem:** a Manchester venue got about 1,000 applications in 24 hours <https://thecaterer.com/news/manchester-20-stories-job-applications>; owners complain about no-shows; screening is done by hand.
**Why now:** no trigger, steady demand.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: reachable employer counts not gathered (unknown)
- Money flowing: Hirevire $13k MRR, Textwork $2.8k MRR, Stripe-verified <https://trustmrr.com/category/recruiting>
- Competitors: Hirevire (video, not WhatsApp); Recruitera (MENA ATS, WhatsApp, prices hidden) <https://recruitera.ai/blog/whatsapp-recruiting-high-volume-hiring-mena>; Workstream, Fountain, Paradox (sales-led, custom pricing)
- The gap: a self-serve, per-role price for single-site employers
**Our angle:** WhatsApp-native, bilingual, $29 a role, no ATS to learn; fits our WhatsApp bot experience.

### 4. Shopify checkout-redirect monitor for agencies · 52/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** E-commerce
**What it is:** for agencies running 10 to 200 Shopify stores: a headless browser adds a product and clicks checkout from several pages and devices every 15 minutes, alerts if it lands off the store or Shopify domain, and diffs third-party scripts daily.
**Who pays:** Shopify agencies and Plus merchants, worldwide.
**The problem:** 10 to 18 Oct, compromised Hulk Product Options, Selleasy and Swym Wishlist Plus redirected checkout buttons to fake checkouts; earlier Qikify (Jul) and Orichi (Sep) <https://letsmetrix.com/blogs/shopify-checkout-hijacking-october-2026>. An Australian merchant was told to stay shut <https://www.itnews.com.au/news/shopify-tells-australian-store-to-stay-shut-after-hijacked-extensions-divert-shoppers-629693>
**Why now:** attacks this month; Shopify promised tighter code limits and IP-locked keys with no dates.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: affected store count unknown
- Money flowing: Bloodhound $49 to $249/month (1 review) <https://apps.shopify.com/bloodhound>; Checkout Sentinel free/$9 (0 reviews) <https://apps.shopify.com/checkout-sentinel>
- Competitors: Bloodhound (no redirect claim); Checkout Sentinel (no hijack claim); Valmont Redirect Guard (now unavailable); Checkly free tier (DIY scripts)
- The gap: multi-store redirect detection plus script diff for agencies
**Our angle:** agency dashboard, randomised device and geo checks, one alert per store.
