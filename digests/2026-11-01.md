### 1. UK third-party harassment evidence file · 63/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** HR
**What it is:** one record of the reasonable steps taken: third-party risk assessment per site, QR incident log, policy and training sign-off, dated evidence pack. Returning (VALIDATE 66 on 29 Sep).
**Who pays:** UK customer-facing employers with 5 to 250 staff (pubs, cafés, shops, dental and vet practices, gyms, care agencies) and the HR consultants who serve them.
**The problem:** since 30 Oct employers must take all reasonable steps to prevent sexual harassment and are liable when third parties harass staff <https://www.business.gov.uk/campaign/employment-changes/employers/workplace-sexual-harassment-and-third-party-harassment/?lang=en-gb>. NHS staff reported almost 24,000 incidents by patients and the public in 2022 to 2025 <https://culture-shift.co.uk/resources/third-party-harassment-toolkit>
**Why now:** duty in force 30 Oct 2026 (England, Wales, Scotland) <https://www.withersworldwide.com/en-gb/insight/read/significant-changes-to-harassment-law-in-the-uk-from-30-october-2026>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: UK customer-facing employer count not checked (unknown)
- Money flowing: law-firm packages and HR platforms sell this; no public prices (unknown)
- Competitors: Saferspace (reporting app, no public price) <https://workplacejournal.co.uk/2025/05/educationscape-backs-saferspace-app-as-new-harassment-laws-hit-uk-employers/>; Culture Shift (demo only); Breathe HR and Citation (free templates); Hill Dickinson (price on request)
- The gap: none of the four pages read offers risk assessment, customer incident log and sign-off in one self-serve product at a public price
**Our angle:** self-serve, £24 a month, live record instead of a template; resold by HR consultants.

### 2. ADM notice register, Australia and California · 59/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Compliance
**What it is:** list the tools that make or shape decisions about people, send each vendor a one-link questionnaire, and get draft notice text for a reviewer: Australian privacy-policy section and California pre-use notice. Returning merge (AU kit 58 on 31 Oct).
**Who pays:** Australian APP entities (generally over A$3m turnover) and CCPA-covered US firms using automated tools for hiring, housing, lending, education or healthcare decisions; their privacy consultants.
**The problem:** AU policies must describe automated decisions from 10 Dec <https://www.allens.com.au/insights-news/insights/2026/10/mandatory-automated-decision-making-disclosure-requirements-are-coming-key-takeaways-from-the-oaic-guidance/>. California needs pre-use notice, opt-out and access from 1 Jan 2027 <https://secureprivacy.ai/blog/cpra-admt-regulations-2026-what-california-businesses-must-do>
**Why now:** 10 Dec 2026 and 1 Jan 2027; CA risk assessments follow by 31 Dec 2027
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: covered business counts unknown
- Money flowing: Secure Privacy sells an ADMT registry (no price); consultants paid hourly (unknown)
- Competitors: Secure Privacy, OneTrust (enterprise), law firms, AU policy generators (ADM coverage unchecked)
- The gap: collecting vendor facts is the slow step per AU commentary
**Our angle:** one inventory feeding two jurisdictions' drafts; never a legal opinion.

### 3. EUDR statement desk for small EU importers · 53/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Logistics
**What it is:** send suppliers one link to upload plot coordinates and documents, validate them against the official rules, file the due-diligence statement (or simplified declaration) through the EU system's API, and keep reference numbers for buyers.
**Who pays:** EU micro and small operators that first place coffee, cocoa, wood, rubber, leather, soy or palm products on the EU market (for example direct-trade coffee roasters, small furniture importers).
**The problem:** each first placement needs a statement with 6-decimal coordinates and polygons for plots over 4 ha <https://eudr.webcloud.ec.europa.eu/tracesnt/help/eudr-documentation/operator/index.html>
**Why now:** large and medium from 30 Dec 2026, micro and small from 30 Jun 2027 <https://vinciworks.com/blog/eudr-delayed-again-what-changed-what-is-now-fixed-and-what-businesses-should-do-in-2026/>; system reopened July <https://www.atibt.org/en/news/13902/eudr-reopening-of-the-eudr-information-system-traces>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of micro and small operators unknown
- Money flowing: PwC from €990/month <https://store.pwc.de/en/products/check-your-value-chain/deforestation-regulation-manager>; Coolset SME plan (custom price); IntegrityNext Starter (fixed annual, not shown)
- Competitors: TRACES manual (free, slow); Coolset (sales call); osapiens (demo); PwC (price)
- The gap: no public flat self-serve price found for operators with a few suppliers
**Our angle:** €49 a month, files via the official API, no demo call.
