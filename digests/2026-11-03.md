### 1. 2027 carrier re-cost and box auditor · 65/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Logistics
**What it is:** upload last quarter's shipping labels; see what each SKU, box and service costs under 2027 USPS, UPS and FedEx rates, plus box swaps that cut billed weight. Returning merge (box auditor 64 on 21 Oct, FedEx report 48 on 13 Oct).
**Who pays:** US e-commerce sellers and small 3PLs shipping about 200 to 5,000 parcels a month on Pirate Ship, ShipStation, Shopify or eBay labels.
**The problem:** small shippers' real increases beat the 5.9% headline <https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/fedexs_2027_rate_hike_set_to_outpace_5.9_headline_for_many_shippers>. A 2 lb Zone 8 FedEx parcel goes $32.62 to $43.18 <https://www.cahoot.ai/fedex-2027-rate-increase/>. eBay sellers call the USPS round-up "phantom weight" <https://community.ebay.com/forum/shipping-57923/topic/heads-up-usps-rounding-up-hidden-rate-hike-468569/>
**Why now:** USPS weekly fuel surcharge from Jan 2027, announced 26 Oct <https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/usps-to-debut-weekly-fuel-fee-in-2027-matching-fedex-ups-practices/872778/>; UPS 21 Dec; FedEx 4 Jan, zones 1 Feb <https://supplychaindive.com/news/fedex-preps-59-rate-hike-surcharge-increases-for-2027/830903>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: US small-shipper count unknown
- Money flowing: Refund Retriever (refund share, 57 reviews); ShipScience advisory $350/h
- Competitors: Reveel GRI tool (demo only); ShipScience (service); DimenAI $9 to $49/mo, 0 reviews; Box Smart $10/mo, 1 review
- The gap: no self-serve tool found that re-costs label history under 2027 rates (absence not proof)
**Our angle:** five minutes, one store, $29, before January.
