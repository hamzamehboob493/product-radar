**Why now:** accredited certification of new installs from 13 Feb 2027 (same NSW page). Soft trigger.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: 2,950 AU fire-protection businesses in 2026 <https://www.ibisworld.com/australia/number-of-businesses/fire-protection-services/5424/>
- Money flowing: UK model FireCert $5,122 MRR, 43 subs at £79/£129, Stripe-verified, founded Feb 2026 <https://trustmrr.com/startup/firecert>
- Competitors: Uptick (quote, 97 reviews, 4.7); FireMate (quote, 3.5 stars); Fire Inspect Hub, InspectOps, Standara (prices unknown); SafetyCulture forms (free to £18/seat)
- The gap: a published flat price for micro firms (unverified until newer rivals are checked)
**Our angle:** self-serve, A$99 flat, no sales call, report sent before leaving site.

**Why now:** NY signed 28 Oct; NY and WA both apply 1 Jan 2027, alongside California.
**Market analysis:**

- Money flowing: age-assurance vendors charge per check (Didit $0.10, 500 free/mo; Yoti quote)
- Competitors: DIY ("a weekend of work", one founder) <https://www.producthunt.com/p/murror/california-s-companion-chatbot-law-has-no-small-dev-exemption>; Didit, Yoti, k-ID for age checks only

**Our angle:** one config for CA, NY, WA, maintained as states add rules.

- Competitors: Link My Books (from $24/mo, no threshold view seen); A2X (from $29); Taxomate (from $19); spreadsheets
