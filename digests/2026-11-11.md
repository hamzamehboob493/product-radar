### 2. UK CBAM records ledger · 63/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Compliance
**What it is:** a self-serve ledger for UK importers of steel, aluminium and other CBAM goods: log each import, track HMRC's £50k tests, and collect supplier emissions evidence. Records only, no tax advice.
**Who pays:** UK SME importers near or below £50k of CBAM goods a year, and small customs brokers.
**The problem:** from 1 Jan 2027 importers must keep records for every CBAM good (code, value, weight, supplier verification) for 6 years, £500 penalty <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/keeping-records-for-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam>. Liability is tested monthly on a rolling 12 months <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/work-out-the-date-youll-need-to-register-for-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-cbam>
**Why now:** UK CBAM starts 1 Jan 2027; registration by 31 Jan 2028, first return 31 May 2028.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: HMRC estimates about 10,000 businesses significantly affected <https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-of-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism>
- Money flowing: Dubrink £1,990 to £2,990/yr; CBAMable £229 to £999/mo for brokers <https://cbamguide.com/learn/uk-cbam/services/>
- Competitors: Dubrink (free only if suppliers pay); CBAMable (broker-only); CarbonChain (demo only); The Sustainability Cloud (€60 to €200/mo, EU only on its pricing page)
- The gap: no self-serve UK tool under £1,990/yr found
**Our angle:** £29/mo, set up in an afternoon, no sales call.
