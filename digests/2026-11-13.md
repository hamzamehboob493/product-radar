### 1. Shopify usage-billing guard · 65/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** SaaS growth
**What it is:** an SDK and dashboard for Shopify app developers who bill by usage: per-store caps, holds on new stores, burn-and-uninstall alerts, recorded vs billed usage check.
**Who pays:** solo and small-studio Shopify app developers with usage or hybrid pricing, worldwide.
**The problem:** "bad actors who create a fresh store" and leave; "Stores consuming usage without valid payment method is a big issue!"; Shopify is "not exploring updating the Billing API" <https://community.shopify.dev/t/shopify-app-pricing-the-next-generation-of-app-billing/34221?page=3>
**Why now:** Mantle's billing product closed 30 Sep, data destroyed <https://docs.heymantle.com/wind-down>; Shopify announced annual plans with usage charges on 11 Nov.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: 17,891 apps, 11,352 developers (May 2026, third-party) <https://www.appjubilee.io/shopify-app-store-report-2026>; share on usage billing unknown
- Money flowing: Mantle served "hundreds of" app businesses, priced by revenue <https://www.adsx.com/blog/mantle-shutting-down-shopify-app-partners>
- Competitors: Marmeto Elevate (free, no guard); Letsmetrix (free, "isn't a billing platform") <https://letsmetrix.com/heymantle-alternatives>; Baremetrics (priced by MRR, no usage features); DIY scripts
- The gap: nobody sells usage-charge protection; developers build their own
**Our angle:** the guard, not another MRR dashboard.

- Money flowing: MSPs buy multi-tenant tools (Inforcer, Augmentt, CoreView); prices unverified
- Competitors: Lighthouse (free, no credits); Inforcer Copilot Manager (quote, adoption only) <https://www.inforcer.com/copilot-manager>; admin center cost pages (one tenant at a time)
- The gap: no cross-tenant credit and spend view found
**Our angle:** credits only, per-tenant price, set up in an hour.

**Market analysis:**
- Market size: UK Shopify store count not checked
- Money flowing: shipping-estimate apps charge $8 to $49/mo but have 0 to 3 reviews <https://apps.shopify.com/shipping-shock-preventer>
- Competitors: Shipping-Shock Preventer (free to $49, estimate only); ShipRate ($15 to $100, no collection pages); Calcurates ($7.99 to $29.99, no total); free theme snippet
- The gap: no listing mentions the CMA or shows a total on collection pages
**Our angle:** the compliance-framed app, UK first, AU next.
