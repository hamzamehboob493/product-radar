### 1. UK subscription notice engine · 59/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Compliance · Returning (59 on 23 Oct)
**What it is:** sends the UK subscription notices Stripe Billing doesn't, hosts a cancel button and keeps proof for years. No legal advice.
**Who pays:** UK-facing consumer subscription businesses on Stripe, 50 to 20,000 subscribers, including sellers outside the UK.
**The problem:** from 1 Jan sellers must send a notice on day 1 of every renewal cooling-off period, an end-of-contract notice, and a reminder every 6 months on rolling plans. Customers must be able to cancel by button or form, and "email alone" is not enough <https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-11-12-are-you-compliant-uk-cma-unveils-guidance-on-new-subscription-rules>. Stripe logs emails for 60 days <https://docs.stripe.com/billing/revenue-recovery/customer-emails>
**Why now:** the regulations were laid in Parliament, Cooley reported on 12 Nov. The CMA fined Wayfair, Appliances Direct and Gold's Gym under the same consumer law in October <https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/regulatory-outlook-october-2026-consumer-law>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: number of UK merchants on Stripe Billing unknown
- Money flowing: two Shopify apps charge $9 to $19/mo, both with 0 reviews <https://apps.shopify.com/subguard> <https://apps.shopify.com/uk-subscription-guard>
- Competitors: Stripe native reminders (free; no cooling-off notice); Paddle (UK reminders only for terms of 6 months or more) <https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/your-customers/enhanced-billing-notifications-location-list>; SubGuard and Subnotice (Shopify only)
- The gap: none of them sends the cooling-off or end-of-contract notices, and none keeps proof past 60 days
**Our angle:** the notices Stripe skips, proof for years, off Shopify.

### 2. WhatsApp applicant screener · 58/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** HR · Returning (53 on 31 Oct)
**What it is:** candidates tap a WhatsApp link and answer 5 to 8 screening questions by text or voice note, in English, Arabic or Urdu. The employer gets a scored shortlist and books interviews. A human makes every decision.
**Who pays:** hourly-hiring employers and small staffing agencies in the UK, UAE and KSA, plus Pakistani overseas employment promoters.
**The problem:** "Blue-collar and frontline candidates often don't have an actively-monitored email address at all" <https://recruitera.ai/blog/whatsapp-recruiting-high-volume-hiring-mena>
**Why now:** no trigger; demand is steady. Hirevire, the closest proven model, grew 37% in a month.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: buyer count unknown. Build the list from the Bureau of Emigration's licensed promoters and UK/UAE agency listings
- Money flowing: Hirevire $14,315 MRR, 162 subscriptions, $49 to $249/mo, Stripe-verified <https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire?metric=mrr>; Recruitera charges $150/mo plus Meta fees for its WhatsApp add-on <https://recruitera.ai/recruitera-ai-whatsapp-hiring-high-volume>
- Competitors: Hirevire (link-based, no WhatsApp); Recruitera (only inside its own ATS, focused on Egypt); Recruit Bud AI (India only, 0 reviews); manual WhatsApp plus Google Form
- The gap: no self-serve, WhatsApp-native screener for small employers was found
**Our angle:** WhatsApp voice notes, three languages, priced at Hirevire's entry level.

### 4. Harvest exit kit · 56/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Productivity · New variant of the Harvest exit scheduler (58 on 9 Oct)
**What it is:** moves every hour, client, project and invoice out of Harvest into Clockify, Toggl, Invoice Ninja or a portable archive, then shows a report proving the totals match.
**Who pays:** freelancers, consultancies and small agencies (1 to 30 users) facing a Harvest renewal at several times the old price. US, UK, EU, AU.
**The problem:** reported renewals of about $130 rising to $2,110/mo <https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/blog/harvest-pricing-change-sends-some-renewals-up-1-500>. A 9 Nov HN thread with 439 points has users citing increases of "about 800%" <https://zeli.app/story/50420035>
**Why now:** Harvest reprices accounts at renewal, so new cases arrive every month. Clockify's free plan was also reportedly cut (details unverified) <https://forum.clockify.me/t/death-of-the-free-plan/10006>
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: Harvest says 70,000+ companies (reported); share hit by the increases unknown
- Money flowing: none proven for migration tools; the only evidence is the Harvest bills themselves
- Competitors: Harvest's own export (files only); Clockify importer (free, invoices not mentioned); Toggl importer (paid plan, past 12 months only, no invoices) <https://support.toggl.com/importing-data-into-toggl-track>; TimeCamp (free)
- The gap: history older than 12 months, invoice records with paid status, and proof that totals match
**Our angle:** destination-neutral, full history, reconciled to the cent.

### 5. Gadget migration kit · 54/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** Developer tools
**What it is:** scans a Shopify app built on Gadget, rewrites the Gadget-specific code into a plain Shopify app on a database we set up (Prisma/Postgres), and exports its data.
**Who pays:** Shopify app developers and agencies with apps on Gadget.
**The problem:** Gadget shuts on 31 Mar 2027. Moving off it is "a code rebuild, not a file copy"; xCloud says it "has no import tool for Gadget apps" <https://xcloud.host/docs/migrate-from-gadget-to-xcloud/>
**Why now:** no new apps since 9 Nov; support ends at year end <https://xcloud.host/move-shopify-app-off-gadget/>. Shopify's expiring-token deadline (1 Jan 2027) lands at the same time.
**Market analysis:**
- Market size: Gadget's showcase lists 87 App Store apps <https://gadget.dev/built-with-gadget>; the true total is unknown and likely higher
- Money flowing: Gadget plans started at $35/mo plus usage (reported); no paid migration offer found
- Competitors: Gadget's own tools (code export only; promised migration tools not yet out); xCloud (free help, hosting from $5/mo, no converter); manual rebuild with AI coding tools
- The gap: automated code conversion
**Our angle:** the only codemod, plus a ready Shopify backend template.
