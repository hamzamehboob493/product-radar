### 1. LS Licence Bridge · 70/100 · VALIDATE (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Micro (build ≤5 days, launch by day 10) · **Sector:** Developer tools
**What it is:** A hosted API that answers exactly like Lemon Squeezy's licence endpoints, imports existing keys and activations, and links them to the seller's new payment provider (Polar, Creem, Dodo, Paddle).
**Who pays:** Solo and small software sellers (desktop apps, WordPress, Figma, Obsidian plugins) who used LS licence keys. Worldwide, most of all outside Stripe Managed Payments countries.
**The problem:** LS payments, including renewals, stop 31 Mar 2027, "will not be extended". Managed Payments has no licence keys, file delivery or affiliates; migration "can take up to eight weeks". <https://techstartups.com/?p=140481> (16 Nov, third-party summary)
**Why now:** Shutdown announced 16 Nov; Stripe tools arrive 2 Dec; LS says start by mid-January.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: LS merchants "thousands" (no exact count). Estimate: about 400 use licence keys (assumption, not data)
- Money: buyers are paying software sellers with a hard date
- Competitors: Dodo free CLI (moves keys, not activations, no compatible API) <https://docs.dodopayments.com/migrate-to-dodo> · Creem 3.9% + 40¢ (does not move keys) <https://docs.creem.io/getting-started/migrate.md> · PayRequest €0 to €20/mo, "not a drop-in" · Freemius (price unknown)
- Gap: nobody offers an LS-compatible API or moves activations. Polar's migration doc lists LS as "pending" <https://handbook.polar.sh/engineering/design-documents/merchant-migrations.md>
**Our angle:** Change one base URL, keep every key and activation, pick any provider.

### 2. CPSC Registry bulk builder for Shopify · 62/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** E-commerce sellers
**What it is:** Upload a Shopify product export and lab PDFs; get a CPSC Product Registry bulk CSV with gaps flagged, then write the returned IDs back into Shopify in bulk.
**Who pays:** Small US importers on Shopify selling regulated consumer or children's products.
**The problem:** CPSC eFiling is mandatory since 8 Jul 2026 with no de minimis exemption <https://zonos.com/docs/guides/cpsc-efiling>. Shopify stores the 3 IDs per product only, no variants <https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/cpsc>. Reported held shipments and storage fees <https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/10/cpsc-efiling-mandate-small-importers-guide>.
**Why now:** Foreign-trade-zone entries must comply from 8 Jan 2027.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: number of affected Shopify importers unknown
- Money: penalties up to $120,500 per violation (reported); lab eFiling services exist
- Competitors: Eurofins/SGS/Intertek (enterprise, price unknown) · Zonos Agency Check (demo only) · RecallDocket $0 to $49/mo (recalls only)
- Gap: Registry has CSV upload and an API <https://www.sgs.com/en-ie/news/2026/10/cc2026q3-11-questions-to-improve-efiling-data-management-for-the-us-cpsc-product-registry>; nothing found links it to Shopify
**Our angle:** Self-serve, catalog-native, published price.

### 3. UK holiday-pay audit · 61/100 · WATCHLIST (card 1 of 2)
**Track:** Bigger build (build 1 to 4 weeks, launch by day 30) · **Sector:** HR
**What it is:** Upload payroll and rota exports (BrightPay, Sage, Xero UK); get a report flagging irregular-hours workers without 12.07% rolled-up pay, holiday pay missing regular overtime or commission, and gaps in the 6-year holiday records.
**Who pays:** UK SMB employers with hourly staff, and the payroll bureaux and accountants who serve them.
**The problem:** The Fair Work Agency intends to enforce holiday pay from Apr 2027: penalty 200% of underpayment, cap £20,000 per worker, public naming <https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/09/16/the-fair-work-agencys-first-delivery-plan>. No penalty if corrected before an investigation; records duty live since Apr 2026 <https://www.birketts.co.uk/legal-update/employment-rights-act-2025-holiday-pay-enforcement/>.
**Why now:** About 4 months of runway; start date "TBC 2027" per Birketts.
**Market analysis:**
- Size: unknown count; estimate needed
- Money: penalty exposure; consultancy audits (price unverified)
- Competitors: BrightPay, Sage, Xero UK, Breathe, BrightHR calculate going forward but do not audit history (prices not checked) · FWA free calculator coming (single calculations)
- Gap: no self-serve bulk historical auditor found
**Our angle:** "Fix it before they look": one file in, one gap report out.
